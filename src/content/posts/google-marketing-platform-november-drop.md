The Google Marketing Platform November drop went live on November 18, 2026. In a [post on Google's blog](https://blog.google/products/marketingplatform/360/google-marketing-platform-drop-november/), Marta Martinez, Managing Director of Google Marketing Platform, lists new inventory and audiences in Display & Video 360, aimed at the holiday shopping season.

The main additions are Spotify and Pinterest inventory, plus Loblaw audiences for retail media in Canada. They matter most to agencies and brands that buy programmatic media through DV360, and to Search Ads 360 users planning holiday bids.

Google also has a separate help page of DV360 changes due in November, and one of them moves where brand safety settings live.

![Google Marketing Platform November drop: what is new in DV360](/images/infographics/ig-google-marketing-platform-november-drop-1.webp)

## Spotify and Pinterest come to Display & Video 360

Google writes that "Display & Video 360 buyers in the U.S. and India can now maximize the impact and resonance of video and audio ads across Spotify's inventory" using Google Audiences. It is the only item in the drop that names India.

Pinterest is the second addition. Google says advertisers "can now access premium video and display inventory alongside high-intent audiences from Pinterest" through programmatic buying in DV360. The post does not name the countries for Pinterest, and neither item carries a beta label.

## Loblaw audiences in Commerce Media Suite

For retail media, Google adds Loblaw, which it calls Canada's largest grocery retailer. Advertisers can reach Loblaw Advance audiences and "prove sales" with closed loop, SKU level measurement.

These audiences sit in Google's Commerce Media Suite in Display & Video 360, next to Walmart, Costco and other major retailers. If you sell through Canadian grocery, this connects your DV360 spend to product level sales.

## Vertical video and Search Ads 360 bidding

The post also links back to [Vertical Video Unification](https://blog.google/products/marketingplatform/360/vertical-video-unification/), launched on September 28, 2026. Google says it lets advertisers buy and measure vertical video from many publishers in one workstream in DV360. This month's post cites Ford, which "drove a 26% lift in unique reach and a 19% reduction in cost per unique user."

For Search Ads 360, Google shares holiday bidding best practices under a formula it calls "Fuel It, Power It, Maximize It." The details sit in an image carousel in the post, so check the original for the specific bid combinations.

## Other Google Marketing Platform changes in November

A [Display & Video 360 help page on Q4 2026 updates](https://support.google.com/displayvideo/answer/18787789?hl=en-IE), last updated October 16, 2026, lists more changes for this month:

| Change | When | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Advertiser level Brand Control Center deprecated | November 2026 | Manage brand safety under Advertiser settings > Targeting rules; partner level Brand Control Center stays |
| Amerilink renamed Open Audiences | November 2026 | Reporting dimension name changes; delivery not affected |
| Floodlight uses Google signed in data signals | November 2026 | Recovers unattributed cross device web conversions in DV360, SA360 and CM360 reports; no action needed |
| Business name and logo required for YouTube line items | October 12, 2026 | Applies to all YouTube line items, including brand auction and instant deals |

Google says the Brand Control Center change "won't affect existing brand suitability protections, campaign delivery, or reporting." Our view: because Floodlight will count conversions it missed before, reported conversion totals may rise without any change in real sales.

## What DV360 and SA360 buyers should do now

![Google Marketing Platform November drop: checklist for programmatic buyers](/images/infographics/ig-google-marketing-platform-november-drop-2.webp)

These steps are Brandella recommendations, not Google guidance.

1. If you advertise in the US or India, ask your DV360 team or agency about Spotify inventory with Google Audiences for audio and video campaigns.
2. Test Pinterest inventory in a small line item with clear goals, and compare it with your existing display and video results.
3. Brands selling through Loblaw should review Loblaw Advance audiences in Commerce Media Suite and agree how SKU level sales will be reported.
4. Move your brand safety checks to Advertiser settings > Targeting rules and update any internal guides that still point to the advertiser level Brand Control Center.
5. Add a note in reports for the Floodlight change, so a jump in attributed conversions is not mistaken for better campaign results.
6. Rename Amerilink to Open Audiences in any saved reports, filters or dashboards.
7. Set default business names and logos at advertiser level since Google now requires them on every YouTube line item.

## FAQ about the Google Marketing Platform November drop

### Is Spotify inventory in DV360 available in India?

Yes. Google says Display & Video 360 buyers in the US and India can use Google Audiences on Spotify video and audio ads.

### Do I need to change anything for the Brand Control Center update?

Yes, if your team uses the advertiser level Brand Control Center. Google says brand safety is managed under Advertiser settings > Targeting rules, while the partner level Brand Control Center keeps working.

### Will the Floodlight change affect my conversion numbers?

Google says Floodlight will use Google signed in data signals to recover conversions that were not attributed before. No action is needed. In our view, reported conversions may rise as a result.

### Which retailers are in Google's Commerce Media Suite?

Google names Loblaw as the new addition, alongside Walmart, Costco and other major retailers.
