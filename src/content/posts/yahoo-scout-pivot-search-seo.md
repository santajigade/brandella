Yahoo Scout, Yahoo's AI answer engine, left beta on November 10, 2026 and now sits inside a rebuilt Yahoo Search. The headline feature is Pivot Search, which lets people move between classic search results and a Scout AI answer on the same page.

You can try it at [Yahoo Search](https://search.yahoo.com/). The details are in Yahoo's official press release, [After Delivering More Than 3 Billion Answers, Yahoo Scout Exits Beta to Underpin All of Yahoo Search](https://www.yahooinc.com/press/after-delivering-more-than-3-billion-answers-yahoo-scout-exits-beta-to-underpin-all-of-yahoo-search). Yahoo says Scout has delivered more than 3 billion answers since it launched in the first quarter of 2026.

Yahoo is a small share of search for most sites, but it is not nothing. Yahoo calls itself the third largest search engine in the US, and this launch is one more AI answer surface that can send, or keep, your traffic. Here is what changed and what to check this week.

![Yahoo Scout launch in numbers: answers delivered, markets and features](/images/infographics/ig-yahoo-scout-pivot-search-seo-1.webp)

## What Yahoo launched on November 10

The release bundles several pieces. Scout is now fully part of Yahoo Search instead of a separate beta. Yahoo says it used the beta period to improve answer quality, accuracy and speed, and to make sure every response carries prominent links to its sources.

Yahoo also says those links are more prominent than in other AI answer engines, and that they are meant to send traffic back to publishers. That is Yahoo's claim, not an independent measurement, so treat it as something to test with your own analytics.

The release was also syndicated by [Editor and Publisher](https://www.editorandpublisher.com/stories/after-delivering-more-than-3-billion-answers-yahoo-scout-exits-beta-to-underpin-all-of-yahoo,264423). Eric Feng, senior vice president and general manager of Yahoo Research Group, said: "We've brought Yahoo Scout and Yahoo Search together to give people both, with the open web at the center."

## How Pivot Search works

Pivot Search is described by Yahoo as a ground-up rebuild of Yahoo Search. It evaluates each query and lets the user switch between traditional results and a Scout answer without leaving the experience.

For some query types, Yahoo's search suggestions offer a Scout answer directly. The examples Yahoo gives are comparing two options and following step-by-step instructions. [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/yahoo-pivot-search-yahoo-scout-42501.html) also reported a Yahoo executive saying "Search is about more than AI."

For SEOs, that query split matters. Comparison and how to content is exactly the kind of page that AI answers summarize, so those pages are the ones most likely to be cited, or skipped, inside a Scout answer.

## Scout Memories, Scout Everywhere and the app

Three more features came with the launch:

- **Scout Memories** remembers details from past Scout activity to personalize future answers. Users can view, delete or opt out at any time.
- **Scout Everywhere** puts Scout inside Yahoo Finance, Yahoo Sports and Yahoo News, with Yahoo Shopping and more listed as coming soon. Users can ask questions without leaving the page.
- **The Scout mobile app** mixes classic search and AI answers with trending topics and weekday audio news digests from Yahoo News. It supports live spoken conversations with a choice of three voices, and photos can be added to a conversation.

Yahoo also says US desktop users can pick Yahoo Scout in Firefox's built-in AI sidebar, and that its first national brand campaign for Scout starts the week of Thanksgiving.

## Where Yahoo Scout is available

| Feature | Where it is available |
|---|---|
| Pivot Search (new Yahoo Search) | Desktop and mobile web in the US, UK, Europe and Switzerland |
| Yahoo Scout app | US, on iOS and Android |
| Scout in Firefox AI sidebar | US desktop users |
| Scout Everywhere | Yahoo Finance, Sports and News now, Shopping coming soon |

Source: Yahoo press release, November 10, 2026. India is not on the list for Pivot Search, so Indian sites will mostly feel this through US, UK and European visitors.

## What SEOs should do now about Yahoo Scout

![Yahoo Scout SEO checklist: five steps to take this week](/images/infographics/ig-yahoo-scout-pivot-search-seo-2.webp)

These steps are Brandella's recommendations, not official Yahoo guidance.

1. **Split Yahoo traffic in analytics.** Create a segment for referrals from yahoo.com and search.yahoo.com and mark November 10 as an annotation. Compare the next four weeks against the four weeks before.
2. **Keep your webmaster tools in order.** Yahoo's release does not explain which index powers the classic results in the rebuilt search, so do not assume anything changed. Make sure your site is verified in Bing Webmaster Tools and your sitemap is submitted. Our [Bing Webmaster Tools guide](/bing-webmaster-tools-complete-guide/) walks through the setup.
3. **Review your comparison and how to pages.** These match the query types Yahoo says trigger Scout answers. Put the direct answer near the top, use clear headings and tables, and keep facts current.
4. **Run test queries yourself.** Search your main topics on Yahoo from a US, UK or EU connection and toggle Pivot Search. Note whether Scout cites your pages and which competitors it links.
5. **Do not rebuild your strategy for one engine.** The same basics that help in Google AI features and ChatGPT apply here. Our [AI search optimization checklist](/ai-search-optimization-checklist-25/) and the guide on [how to increase AI search citations](/how-to-increase-ai-search-citations/) cover them.

## How Yahoo Scout fits the wider AI search picture

Google, Microsoft and OpenAI all now mix links and generated answers, and Yahoo is taking the same route with a stronger promise about source links.
