CPMs on Threads ads are still low, competition is still thin, and the window to build organic-feeling creative before the platform gets crowded is open right now.</p>

<p>Threads launched in July 2023 and spent close to two years as a text-first, ad-free space.</p>

<p>We recently covered <a href="/meta-one-click-conversions-api-pixel-setup/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Meta's one-click Conversions API update</a>, part of the same broader push to make Meta's ad ecosystem easier to run campaigns across. <h2 id="what-just-happened-with-threads-ads">What Just Happened With Threads Ads</h2>
<p>Meta announced the global expansion of Threads ads on January 21, 2026, with the rollout to all users beginning the following week.</p>

<p>According to <a href="https://searchengineland.com/meta-expands-threads-ads-to-all-users-globally-467786" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Search Engine Land's coverage of the announcement</a>, this gives advertisers access to a platform Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has called a potential "next big hit," with ambitions to reach a billion users.</p>

<p>Advertisers can now add Threads as a placement through Advantage+ campaigns or set it up manually in Ads Manager, managed alongside Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp inside the same Business Settings panel, as <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/21/threads-rolls-out-ads-to-all-users-worldwide" target="_blank" rel="noopener">TechCrunch reported</a> when the news broke.</p>

<h2 id="threads-ads-vs-instagram-ads-the-real-differences">Threads Ads vs. Instagram Ads: The Real Differences</h2>
<p>Threads is not simply Instagram with a different feed. The format, the tone, and the competitive landscape around Threads ads are all meaningfully different right now, which is exactly why a copy-paste approach from Instagram tends to underperform.</p>

<div class="bta-era-grid">
<div class="bta-era-card old">
<h3>Instagram Ads</h3>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Visual-first feed spanning Stories, Reels, and Explore</div>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Higher CPMs, typically $6 to $18, due to mature advertiser demand</div>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Polished, produced creative generally performs well</div>
</div>
<div class="bta-era-card new">
<h3>Threads Ads</h3>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Text-first, conversational feed with supporting visuals</div>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Lower CPMs, typically $3 to $8, due to lighter advertiser competition</div>
<div class="bta-era-item">• Creative that mimics an organic thread tends to outperform polished ads</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 id="why-testing-threads-ads-now-makes-sense">Why Testing Threads Ads Now Makes Sense</h2>
<p>Early access to a growing ad platform rarely lasts long. A handful of specific factors make this particular window worth paying attention to.</p>

<div class="bta-factors">
<div class="bta-factor">
<div class="bta-factor-num">1</div>
<div>
<h3>Lower Competition Means Lower CPMs</h3>
<p>With fewer advertisers actively bidding on Threads inventory, current CPMs sit well below Instagram's, according to <a href="https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/meta-threads-carousel-ads-2026-guide" target="_blank" rel="noopener">1ClickReport's 2026 carousel ads guide</a>.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="bta-factor">
<div class="bta-factor-num">2</div>
<div>
<h3>Organic Engagement Is Already Strong</h3>
<p>Threads drives meaningfully higher engagement than X on organic posts, and that advantage appears to carry over into paid placements as well.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="bta-factor">
<div class="bta-factor-num">3</div>
<div>
<h3>Setup Requires No New Learning Curve</h3>
<p>Threads ads run through the same Ads Manager, the same Advantage+ system, and the same targeting tools advertisers already use for Facebook and Instagram.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="bta-factor">
<div class="bta-factor-num">4</div>
<div>
<h3>Your Best Organic Content Is Reusable</h3>
<p>According to <a href="https://postory.io/blog/meta-threads-ads" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Postory's founder-focused guide to Threads ads</a>, the winning creative strategy is mining existing organic posts and reusing those hooks as paid creative, rather than producing new assets from scratch.</p>
</div>
</div>
<div class="bta-factor">
<div class="bta-factor-num">5</div>
<div>
<h3>The Reading Experience Stays Protected</h3>
<p>Meta designed the rollout without unskippable pre-rolls or interruptions in the Following feed, according to <a href="https://lemonadedigitalmedia.com/en/threads-ads-launch-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Lemonade Digital Media's coverage of the launch</a>, which keeps the platform's tone intact for advertisers who match it.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 id="how-to-set-up-your-first-threads-ads-test">How to Set Up Your First Threads Ads Test</h2>
<p>Start inside Meta Ads Manager by creating a new campaign or duplicating an existing Instagram campaign that has performed reasonably well.</p>

<p>In the placements section, add Threads manually rather than relying solely on Advantage+ placements, at least for your first test. This lets you isolate Threads performance data separately from your other Meta placements.</p>

<p>Keep the creative simple. A single image or short video that reads like a genuine thread post, paired with concise primary text between 80 and 160 characters, tends to outperform anything that looks like a traditional ad unit, based on <a href="https://almcorp.com/blog/meta-threads-ads-global-expansion-complete-guide-advertisers-2026/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ALM Corp's format specifications</a>.</p>

<p>Run the test for at least one to two weeks before drawing conclusions, since Meta noted that ad delivery would stay intentionally low while the platform scales its rollout globally, as <a href="https://www.campaignlive.com/article/meta-rolls-ads-threads-globally-introduces-new-ad-formats/1946019" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Campaign US reported</a> following the announcement.</p>

<h2 id="what-this-means-for-your-broader-meta-strategy">What This Means for Your Broader Meta Strategy</h2>
<p>Threads ads are not a replacement for Instagram or Facebook campaigns. They are an additional placement worth testing while the cost-to-reach ratio still favors early movers.</p>

<p>According to <a href="https://www.jumpfly.com/blog/threads-ads-go-global-what-meta-advertisers-need-to-know/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JumpFly's analysis of the global rollout</a>, Threads is included within Advantage+ placements by default.</p>

<p>Many advertisers may already be running Threads impressions without realizing it. Checking your placement breakdown in Ads Manager is a useful first step, even before launching a dedicated test.</p>

<p>Pair any Threads test with solid server-side tracking, since accurate conversion data becomes more important as you spread budget across more placements.</p>

<p>Our recent piece on <a href="/meta-one-click-conversions-api-pixel-setup/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Meta's one-click Conversions API setup</a> is a good next step if your tracking is not already dialed in. For a broader view of how paid platforms are evolving in 2026, see our <a href="/category/paid-media/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">paid media strategy guide</a>.</p>

<h2 id="how-to-measure-threads-ads-performance-correctly">How to Measure Threads Ads Performance Correctly</h2>
<p>Because Threads is a newer placement, it deserves its own performance review rather than being lumped into overall Meta account numbers.</p>

<p>In Ads Manager, break out reporting by placement and isolate Threads specifically. Compare cost per result, click-through rate, and engagement rate against your Instagram benchmarks from the same campaign objective, not against your account-wide average.</p>

<p>Early Threads ads data often looks noisy for the first week or two, simply because delivery volume is lower than on more established placements. Waiting for a meaningful sample size before making budget decisions avoids overreacting to a handful of early results.</p>

<p>If your Threads ads numbers look promising after two to three weeks, consider shifting a modest percentage of your Instagram test budget toward Threads rather than adding entirely new spend.</p>

<p>This keeps your overall Meta ads budget stable while still capturing the lower-CPM advantage Threads currently offers.</p>

<h2 id="common-mistakes-to-avoid-with-threads-ads">Common Mistakes to Avoid With Threads Ads</h2>
<p>The most common mistake advertisers make with Threads ads is treating the placement exactly like Instagram. Reused Instagram creative, without adjustment, tends to feel out of place in a feed built around short, conversational text.</p>

<p>A second common mistake is expecting desktop-level reach. Since Threads ads currently run mobile-only, campaigns planned with a desktop-first mindset will underdeliver relative to expectations.</p>

<p>A third mistake is judging Threads ads performance too early. Meta has been explicit that ad delivery stays intentionally low during the platform's gradual scaling, so a slow first week is expected behavior, not a signal to pause the test.</p>
