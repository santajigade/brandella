ChatGPT Ads reach and frequency reporting is now live. In a product update email sent to advertisers on November 25, 2026, OpenAI said Ads Manager and the Ads API now show how many people saw your ads and how often, plus seven related audience metrics. [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-ads-updates-42623.html) published the email with a screenshot the same day.

The email also covered four smaller changes: a default "Learn more" link on single image ads, Business Agents for some businesses outside the US, a switch to hide sale prices in product feed ads, and clearer rejection messages.

For anyone buying ChatGPT Ads, this is the first time the platform reports unique people rather than only impressions and clicks. You can now see whether a budget is reaching new people or showing the same ad to the same users again.

![ChatGPT Ads product update November 25, 2026: 9 new audience metrics, Learn more link on by default, Business Agents outside the US, policy named on rejected ads](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-ads-reach-frequency-metrics-1.webp)

## The nine ChatGPT Ads reach and frequency metrics

OpenAI gave definitions for two of the nine metrics. Reach is an estimate of how many unique people saw your ads at least once in the selected date range. Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach for the same period.

The other seven were named without definitions: Click Reach, Click Frequency, Total Reach, Total Frequency, Unique CTR, Unique CPC and Cost per 1,000 Reached. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-ad-reports-add-9-metrics-built-on-how-many-people-see-the-ads/) points out that OpenAI has not said how it identifies unique people or whether reach is de-duplicated across campaigns. Until OpenAI documents that, treat the unnamed metrics with care and avoid comparing them one to one with Meta or Google numbers.

To add the columns, open Ads Manager, choose Edit columns, pick the metrics under Ad Viewers and select Save changes.

| Metric | What OpenAI said |
|---|---|
| Reach | Estimated unique people who saw your ads at least once in the date range |
| Frequency | Total impressions divided by Reach for the same period |
| Click Reach, Click Frequency | Named, not defined |
| Total Reach, Total Frequency | Named, not defined |
| Unique CTR, Unique CPC | Named, not defined |
| Cost per 1,000 Reached | Named, not defined |

According to PPC Land, the reporting before this update covered impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, average CPC, average CPM and conversions, with view-through conversions added later for eligible accounts.

## A Learn more link on single image ads

Single image ads now show a "Learn more" link below the description by default. It opens the landing page you already set in Ads Manager, so there is nothing to configure. The email does not say whether you can switch it off.

A new clickable link may change click volume on image ads, so note the date in your reports before reading any jump as a creative win.

## Business Agents outside the US

Eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in supported markets outside the US can now create and publish a Business Agent in the ChatGPT Business Hub. The email does not list those markets.

When Search Engine Roundtable covered the [Business Hub launch](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-business-hub-42505.html) on November 10, Business Agents were limited to eligible US businesses. Advertisers with access who meet ad policy requirements can now add their agent to an eligible campaign in Ads Manager as a Sponsored Business Agent. We explained how claiming a profile works in our guide to [Businesses in ChatGPT and Business Hub agents](/businesses-in-chatgpt-business-hub-agents/).

## Sale price control and clearer rejections

For ad groups that use a product feed, you can now edit the ad group and select "Hide product sale prices" under Product display. The ads then show the regular price, and both prices can stay in the feed.

If an ad is not approved, the review message in Ads Manager now says which policy applies. The Policy Center sits in the dropdown at the bottom left of Ads Manager. OpenAI said the approval guidelines themselves have not changed.

## What ChatGPT advertisers should do now

![What ChatGPT advertisers should do now: add Reach and Frequency columns, set a frequency baseline, mark the Learn more date, check sale prices, read rejected ad policies, check Business Agent access](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-ads-reach-frequency-metrics-2.webp)

These steps are our recommendations, not OpenAI guidance:

1. Add Reach and Frequency to your default column set today, so every report from now on includes them.
2. Pull the last few weeks with the new columns and note the frequency on each campaign. That gives you a baseline before holiday spend peaks.
3. Add a note in your reporting for November 25, 2026, the date the Learn more link appeared, so later click changes are read in context.
4. Check how product feed ads show prices. If a sale price is causing confusion or mismatch with your site, test "Hide product sale prices."
5. Go through any rejected ads and read the policy now named in the review message before you edit and resubmit.
6. If you sell outside the US, check Business Hub for Business Agent access, then look at whether a Sponsored Business Agent fits a campaign.

## FAQ about ChatGPT Ads reach and frequency

### When did reach and frequency arrive in ChatGPT Ads?

OpenAI announced them in a product update email to advertisers on November 25, 2026. They are in Ads Manager and the Ads API.

### How is Frequency calculated in ChatGPT Ads?

Frequency is total impressions divided by Reach for the same date range, according to the OpenAI email.

### Can I turn off the Learn more link?

The email says the link shows by default on single image ads. It does not say whether it can be turned off.

### Which countries can use Business Agents now?

OpenAI says eligible businesses in supported markets outside the US, but the email does not name those markets.

### Did OpenAI change its ad approval rules?

No. OpenAI said the guidelines are unchanged. Rejected ads now just explain which policy applies.
