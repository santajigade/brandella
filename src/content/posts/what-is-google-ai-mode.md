<h2 id="what-is-google-ai-mode-how-to-use-it-and-its-best-features">What Is Google AI Mode, How to Use It, and Its Best Features</h2>
<p>Google AI Mode is changing how we search.</p>
<p>This is not a small update.</p>
<p>It is a structural shift in how answers are delivered.</p>
<p>After announcing it during <strong><a href="https://io.google/" target="_new" rel="noopener">Google I/O</a></strong>, Google began rolling out <a href="/category/artificial-intelligence/">AI Mode</a> as part of its broader Search evolution. Sundar Pichai described it clearly: AI Mode is where Google brings its most advanced AI capabilities directly into Search.</p>
<p>Instead of ten blue links, users now get synthesized answers.</p>
<p>Instead of keywords, users have conversations.</p>
<p>And that changes everything.</p>
<img src="/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Google-AI-Mode.webp" alt="Google AI Mode" width="1000" height="500" />
<h2 id="what-is-google-ai-mode">What Is Google AI Mode?</h2>
<p>Google AI Mode is an AI-powered search experience.</p>
<p>Unlike traditional Google Search, which shows ranked webpages, AI Mode generates structured answers using AI models like <strong><a href="https://deepmind.google/technologies/gemini/" target="_new" rel="noopener">Gemini</a></strong>.</p>
<p>It blends:</p>
<ol>
 	<li>AI-generated summaries</li>
 	<li>Traditional web links</li>
 	<li>Context-aware responses</li>
</ol>
<p>This means when you search for something complex — like comparing smartphones or planning a trip — Google analyzes multiple sources and provides a direct, structured response.</p>
<p>Google has already introduced AI summaries through <strong><a href="https://blog.google/products/search/generative-ai-search/" target="_new" rel="noopener">AI Overviews</a></strong>. AI Mode takes that concept further by making conversational search the core experience.</p>
<p>It also supports multimodal inputs.</p>
<p>You can type, speak, or upload images.</p>
<p>The AI understands context across formats.</p>
<h2 id="how-search-is-changing">How Search Is Changing</h2>
<p>Search is no longer just about keywords.</p>
<p>It is about intent.</p>
<p>Elizabeth Reid, Google’s Head of Search, has explained that users are now asking layered and personal questions instead of short keyword phrases.</p>
<p>Instead of typing “best father’s day gifts,” users might ask:</p>
<p>“What are good gift ideas for my dad who likes fitness and travel?”</p>
<p>AI Mode responds conversationally.</p>
<p>It understands nuance.</p>
<p>It remembers follow-up questions.</p>
<p>This is closer to how people use tools like <strong><a href="https://openai.com/chatgpt" target="_new" rel="noopener">ChatGPT</a></strong>, but directly inside Google Search.</p>
<p>Even if you do not enable AI Mode, Google has confirmed many AI capabilities will gradually merge into standard Search.</p>
<p>This is not an experiment.</p>
<p>It is a direction.</p>
<h2 id="how-to-use-google-ai-mode">How to Use Google AI Mode</h2>
<p>AI Mode is currently rolling out in phases. It is officially available for English users in the United States and expanding gradually.</p>
<p>To access it:</p>
<ol>
 	<li>Visit <strong><a href="https://labs.google/" target="_new" rel="noopener">Google Search Labs</a></strong> and enable AI Search features.</li>
 	<li>Search normally on Google.com and select the AI tab if available.</li>
 	<li>Use the Google app and look for AI Mode within Search.</li>
</ol>
<p>Once enabled, you can:</p>
<ol>
 	<li>Ask conversational questions</li>
 	<li>Compare products or services</li>
 	<li>Upload images for visual search</li>
 	<li>Use voice queries</li>
</ol>
<p>AI Mode generates summaries at the top, often with cited sources for deeper reading.</p>
<h2 id="best-features-of-google-ai-mode">Best Features of Google AI Mode</h2>
<p>Google AI Mode is not just a chatbot.</p>
<p>It is a hybrid of AI and Search.</p>
<p>Here are its most important features.</p>
<h3>1. Advanced AI Overviews</h3>
<p>AI Mode summarizes complex topics instantly.</p>
<p>Instead of reading multiple blogs, you receive a concise overview with source links.</p>
<h3>2. Conversational Follow-Ups</h3>
<p>You can ask follow-up questions without repeating context.</p>
<p>The system remembers the thread.</p>
<p>It behaves more like a smart assistant than a search engine.</p>
<h3>3. Multimodal Search</h3>
<p>Powered by Gemini, AI Mode supports text, voice, and image input.</p>
<p>You can upload a product photo or speak a query while driving.</p>
<p>This reflects how people actually search today.</p>
<h3>4. Personalized Suggestions</h3>
<p>With permission, AI Mode can use contextual signals such as your location or activity to provide smarter suggestions.</p>
<p>You remain in control of privacy settings.</p>
<h3>5. Deep Search Capabilities</h3>
<p>For layered queries, AI Mode performs what Google calls deeper reasoning.</p>
<p>It scans multiple sources, synthesizes information, and produces structured comparisons.</p>
<p>This is especially useful for research, financial comparisons, or buying decisions.</p>
<h3>6. Smart Shopping Tools</h3>
<p>AI Mode refines product searches conversationally.</p>
<p>Users can filter by budget, size, features, or style naturally.</p>
<p>Google is also testing virtual try-on features within its AI shopping experience.</p>

<h2 id="google-ai-mode-faqs">Google AI Mode FAQs</h2>
<h3>1. Is Google AI Mode available in India?</h3>
<p>It is rolling out in phases. It is officially live for English-speaking users in the U.S., with gradual expansion.</p>
<h3>2. How do I access Google AI Mode?</h3>
<p>Visit Google Search Labs, enable AI features, or look for the AI tab within Google Search.</p>
<h3>3. What is the difference between AI Mode and regular Search?</h3>
<p>Regular Search shows ranked links. AI Mode generates structured answers and supports conversational follow-ups.</p>
<h3>4. Is AI Mode better than ChatGPT or Gemini?</h3>
<p>It depends on the task. AI Mode integrates AI directly into Search, while tools like ChatGPT are standalone conversational platforms.</p>

<p>Yes. It is currently free for users with a Google account.</p>
