Google Search profiles look like they are opening up well beyond North America and India. On November 22, [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-profiles-global-42591.html) reported that people outside the US, Canada and India can now claim a profile without a VPN. Google has not announced it, and the publication said so.

The reports come from individual users. Clara Soteras said she claimed one in Spain, and Gagan Ghotra said profiles showed up on Australian, Singapore and Dubai Gmail accounts. If you publish content or run a brand with a real social following, it is worth checking whether you can claim yours.

Google says claiming one can create or update a knowledge panel, and that people who follow you are more likely to see your content in Discover.

![Google Search profiles at launch compared with the November 2026 reports](/images/infographics/ig-google-search-profiles-global-rollout-1.webp)

## What Google Search profiles are

Google announced Search profiles on [its official blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/a-new-profile-to-help-publishers-and-creators-highlight-their-work-on-search/) on June 4, 2026, in a post by Search product manager Ibrahim Badr. It calls a profile "a dedicated, shareable space to highlight content across platforms."

Once you claim a profile, you can add an avatar, a bio, your website, your social and video accounts, and other content. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/06/04/google-search-profiles/) described a "Pinned" carousel for short videos from Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, plus a "Latest posts" grid that can include articles.

On mobile, people reach a profile from your knowledge panel, by tapping your name above a card in Discover, or through a direct link. There is also a Follow on Google button.

## How Google Search profiles spread country by country

| Date | What happened | Source |
|---|---|---|
| June 4, 2026 | Launch, US only | Google blog |
| October 6, 2026 | India and Canada added, Brazil "soon" | Robby Stein on X, via Search Engine Roundtable |
| November 22, 2026 | Claims reported in Spain, Australia, Singapore and Dubai | User reports, via Search Engine Roundtable |

The [October expansion](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-profiles-india-canada-brazil-42245.html) came from Robby Stein, VP of Product at Google Search, who wrote that "those in India and Canada are now eligible." No source we read gives a date for Brazil or confirms a global launch.

So treat the November change as a report, not an official rollout. Some countries may still be blocked, and access may differ by account.

## Who qualifies: the follower threshold is unclear

Google's blog post only says profiles are for "publishers and creators with a sizable following on at least one major social or video platform." It gives no number.

At launch, [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-launches-search-profiles-for-creators-with-100k-followers/577983/) reported, citing Google's help page, that you needed 100,000 followers on YouTube, Instagram or X, or 300,000 on TikTok. Search Engine Roundtable's November report says you need at least 10,000 followers on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or X.

We could not confirm which figure Google uses today, or when it changed. The quickest test is to try the claim flow yourself. Search Engine Journal also reported that creators must be 18 or older, though an adult can manage a profile on behalf of a minor.

## How to claim your Google Search profile

Both publications point to the claim page at [profile.google.com/claim](https://profile.google.com/claim). According to Search Engine Journal's launch report:

1. Sign in and link at least one qualifying social or video account.
2. Select "Create Profile."
3. Your handle comes from your most-followed linked account, or the next one if that handle is taken.
4. Changes to your name, social links or bio need Google's approval and show as "Pending" until reviewed.
5. New content from linked platforms usually appears within 24 hours.

The same report says creating a profile does not affect rankings. The benefit is in how you appear in Search and Discover.

## What publishers and creators should do now

These are our recommendations, not Google instructions.

![Five steps to check and claim a Google Search profile](/images/infographics/ig-google-search-profiles-global-rollout-2.webp)

- Try the claim page today. Sign in with the Google account that should own the brand and see whether you are eligible. If your country is blocked, check again in a few weeks.
- Link your most-followed account first, because it sets your handle, so choose the brand account rather than a personal one.
- Every edit to the bio and links goes into review, so get the name, website and social links right first time.
- Look at your knowledge panel afterwards. Google says claiming can create one or update an existing panel with your avatar, latest content and a profile link.
- In Search Console, compare Discover clicks before and after you claim. Branded search data, which we covered in our post on the [Search Analytics API branded queries update](/search-analytics-api-branded-queries/), helps show whether people look for you by name.

## FAQ about Google Search profiles

### Has Google announced a global rollout of Search profiles?

No. As of November 22, Search Engine Roundtable said the wider rollout had not been announced. The evidence is user reports from Spain, Australia, Singapore and Dubai.

### How many followers do I need for a Google Search profile?

Reports differ. At launch Search Engine Journal cited 100,000 on YouTube, Instagram or X and 300,000 on TikTok. The November report says 10,000. Try the claim page to see if you qualify.

### Does a Google Search profile help rankings?

Search Engine Journal's launch report, citing Google's help page, says it does not affect ranking. Google says followers are more likely to see your content in Discover.

### Can a business or publisher claim a profile, or only creators?

Google says profiles are for both publishers and creators with a sizable following on at least one major social or video platform.
