Google Connected Apps now cover groceries, credit checks and tax estimates in the US. In a [post on the Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/connected-apps-holiday-errands/) dated November 20, 2026, Google said "In the U.S., we're rolling out new Connected Apps through Search and the Gemini app", with Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target among the grocery partners.

People can build a grocery order and check out from inside Google, check a credit rating with Experian, or work out a tax refund with H&R Block. Each of those tasks used to send someone to the brand's own website or app.

For retailers and marketers, that moves part of the buying decision into Google's own interface, even though the brand still supplies the product.

![Google Connected Apps partners announced November 20, 2026](/images/infographics/ig-google-connected-apps-target-experian-hr-block-1.webp)

## What Google Connected Apps added on November 20

| Partner | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's, Target | Build a grocery order and check out | Search and the Gemini app |
| Experian | Check a credit rating and shop for a personal loan | Live in Gemini, now rolling out to Search |
| H&R Block | Calculate taxes and estimate a refund | Search |
| Angi, Thumbtack | Link an account to find local pros | Search, "in the coming weeks" |

Google introduced the grocery names with the word "including", so more partners may follow. [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/google-puts-target-and-albertsons-grocery-carts-inside-search-and-gemini/) notes that Albertsons, Safeway and Shaw's are all banners of Albertsons Companies.

On credit, Google wrote: "Experian is already live on the Gemini app, and now we're rolling it out to Search." For taxes: "U.S. users can also turn to H&R Block to calculate their taxes and estimate their refunds right from Search."

## How a grocery order works in AI Mode

PPC Land walked through the example Google shared. A user makes a request in AI Mode, gets suggestions, invokes Target by typing "@Target", confirms and ends up with a pickup cart of 8 items, including bagels, cream cheese and croissants, with a sub-total of $37.49 at a Nashville store. It reports that the example stops at the cart, with no sign-in, payment or pickup slot screen shown.

PPC Land also says Google has not explained how products are ranked, who takes the payment or whether ads can appear in these flows. Until Google publishes more, treat those details as unconfirmed.

It cites its own earlier reporting that Connected Apps in Search are limited to AI Mode, the US, English, signed-in users and one linked app per user. Google's November 20 post does not repeat those limits, so check them against your own tests.

## The Universal Commerce Protocol behind it

Google says these apps are "Powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol". Its [Merchant Center help page on UCP](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/16837055?hl=en) describes the protocol as "a new open standard for agentic commerce" that runs from discovery through checkout and post-purchase support.

UCP-powered checkout puts a Buy button on eligible product listings in AI Mode and Gemini. The merchant stays the seller of record, and shoppers pay with Google Pay using details saved in Google Wallet. The help page says it applies to products eligible in the United States, Canada and Australia, and is open to select merchants through an early access program for now.

Connected Apps launched in July, when Google brought Instacart, Canva and YouTube Music into AI Mode in a post titled [Connect more of your apps to Search](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/connected-apps/). The new partners add grocery checkout, credit and tax tasks.

## Who this affects

Grocery and big box retailers are the most directly affected. If Target or Safeway can fill a cart inside AI Mode, a rival that is missing from Connected Apps may never come up in that conversation.

Financial and tax brands sit close to this too. Experian and H&R Block now meet people at the moment they ask a money question, which used to be a paid search click.

Local service businesses should note Angi and Thumbtack. We covered the earlier [Google AI Mode home services booking](/google-ai-mode-home-services-booking/) links, and account linking in Search pushes that one step further.

## What retailers and marketers should do now

![What to do now about Google Connected Apps](/images/infographics/ig-google-connected-apps-target-experian-hr-block-2.webp)

These steps are Brandella's recommendations, not Google guidance.

1. Test the flows yourself on a signed-in US account. Ask AI Mode and the Gemini app the questions your customers ask, and record which brands appear.
2. Check your Merchant Center feed. Clean titles, prices and availability are the inputs any agent reads first, and the native_commerce attribute controls checkout eligibility under UCP.
3. If you sell in the US, Canada or Australia, read the UCP requirements on the [Universal Commerce Protocol developer page](https://developers.google.com/merchant/ucp) and decide whether to submit the interest form.
4. Separate agent traffic in analytics. Our guide on [Google-Agent traffic](/google-agent-traffic-allow-guide/) explains how Google's agents identify themselves.
5. Watch for purchases that never touch your site. Compare in-store pickup orders and app orders with your web sessions so you notice if part of the journey moves into Google.
6. Review how other agent checkouts work, such as [ChatGPT Wallet](/chatgpt-wallet-ai-agent-purchases/), so your team has one plan for agentic commerce instead of one per platform.

## FAQ about Google Connected Apps

### Which countries get the new Connected Apps?

Google's November 20 post says the rollout is in the US. It gives no dates for other countries.

### Do I need to be a partner to sell through AI Mode checkout?

UCP-powered checkout is open to select merchants through an early access program. Merchants meet the technical requirements, submit an interest form and then complete the implementation.

### Are ads shown inside Connected Apps?

Google has not said. PPC Land reports that a Connected Apps policy bars partners from serving third-party ads through their integrations, but Google's announcement does not cover ads.

### Is this the same as Google agentic checkout?

No. Google's help page says agentic checkout buys on the merchant's own site, while UCP standardizes how an AI agent and the merchant's backend exchange information for discovery and checkout in AI Mode and Gemini.
