Personal Intelligence in AI Mode is getting three more data sources. In a [Google blog post](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/google-search-personal-intelligence-apps/) published on November 23, 2026, Robby Stein, VP of Product for Google Search, said users can start connecting Google Wallet that day, with Google Drive and the Gemini app "following in the coming weeks." They join Gmail, Calendar and Photos, which were already connected.

Every connection is opt in. Once a user turns it on, AI Mode can draw on cards, documents and Gemini chats to tailor answers, and Google says recent search context will also shape more responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode.

For SEO and content teams, that means two people typing the same query can get answers built from very different personal context. Rank checks from a clean browser will tell you less about what a signed in user actually sees.

![Personal Intelligence in AI Mode connected apps: Gmail, Calendar and Photos already connected, Wallet rolling out from November 23, 2026, Drive and Gemini app in the coming weeks, Personalization icon coming soon](/images/infographics/ig-personal-intelligence-ai-mode-wallet-drive-1.webp)

## What each new app adds to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode

Google Wallet is the one rolling out now. Google's examples are mapping out spending and financial milestones, finding ways to trim everyday spending and getting more from credit card rewards such as cash back, travel perks and points, based on "cards linked via Wallet." Google is clear about the limit: "AI Mode can only read information from your accounts; it can't move, manage, or invest your money."

Google Drive comes next. The example Google gives is a personal plan for tax season that pulls from your docs, sheets and PDFs.

The Gemini app connection works in both directions. Google says that in the coming weeks context will be shared between the Gemini app and AI Mode, and that users could already connect their Search history to Personal Intelligence inside the Gemini app.

| App | Status per Google's post |
|---|---|
| Gmail, Calendar, Photos | Already connected |
| Google Wallet | Rolling out from November 23, 2026 |
| Google Drive | Coming in the next few weeks |
| Gemini app | Coming in the next few weeks, context shared both ways |
| Personalization icon to view context | Coming soon, no date |

The post does not say which countries, languages or subscription plans get these features, so do not assume it is live for everyone.

## Recent searches will shape more answers

Separately from the app connections, Google says people will "start seeing more personally relevant responses" in AI Overviews and AI Mode based on their recent search context. The example is coming back to trip planning in a new thread without having to repeat yourself.

Google also says a "Personalization" icon will soon appear below any tailored response so users can see the context AI Mode used. If something is wrong, users can tell AI Mode to change it, as in "I'm no longer a vegetarian."

## How users turn it on and off

Google's steps: open Search Personalization, select Connected Apps and choose the apps to connect, from the [connected apps page](https://myactivity.google.com/search-services/apps). Google says "You choose if and when you opt in, and you can turn those connections on or off at any time." Personalization as a whole can be adjusted or turned off in [Search personalization settings](https://www.google.com/search-personalization).

## Why this matters for SEO and brands

Google has not said how personal context changes which websites appear or get cited in AI Mode, so the points below are our reading, not a Google statement.

A Wallet connection puts card rewards and spending into the answer. Queries such as "best way to pay for flights" or "which card for groceries" may come back shaped by the cards a user already holds. Finance, travel and retail brands should expect less uniform answers on those topics.

Drive gives AI Mode the user's own documents, so for planning queries the user's files sit alongside the open web as context.

And personalised results make standard rank tracking less representative, a problem we also saw with the [Google sign-in wall test](/google-sign-in-wall-rank-trackers/).

## What SEO teams should do now

![What SEO teams should do now: test queries signed in with Wallet, compare with a clean session, review finance, travel and retail pages, keep facts current, watch Search Console, retest when Drive and Gemini arrive](/images/infographics/ig-personal-intelligence-ai-mode-wallet-drive-2.webp)

These steps are our recommendations, not Google guidance:

1. Test your top queries in AI Mode on a signed in test account with Wallet connected, then repeat them in a clean session and compare the answers and cited sources.
2. If you work in finance, travel or retail, review pages on card rewards, budgeting and spending, since those match Google's Wallet examples.
3. Keep facts on those pages specific and current, such as fees, reward rates and dates, so they are worth citing when AI Mode builds a tailored answer.
4. Watch Search Console clicks and impressions for AI Mode heavy queries and note November 23, 2026 as the Wallet rollout date.
5. Add the Drive and Gemini app launches to your calendar when Google confirms them, and run the same tests again.

## FAQ about Personal Intelligence in AI Mode

### Which apps can connect to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode?

Gmail, Calendar and Photos already could. Google Wallet started rolling out on November 23, 2026, and Drive and the Gemini app are due in the coming weeks.

### Can AI Mode spend or move money through Google Wallet?

No. Google says AI Mode can only read information from your accounts and cannot move, manage or invest money.

### Is Personal Intelligence on by default?

No. Users opt in under Search Personalization, Connected Apps, and can switch each connection off at any time.

### Is it available in every country?

Google's post does not list countries, languages or plans, so availability is not confirmed.
