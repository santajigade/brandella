Google is opening its Gemini Business Profile tools to more owners. According to [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/google-gemini-business-profile-workspace-2/596120), Google's November Small Business Bulletin, posted on November 5, 2026 in the [Business Profile Community](https://support.google.com/business/thread/476642299/google-small-business-bulletin-%F0%9F%8E%81-holiday-checklist-new-features?hl=en), says owners who sign in with Google Workspace accounts can now use Gemini to manage their profile, and describes this as rolling out now.

There is a catch. Google's own [Gemini and Business Profile help page](https://support.google.com/business/answer/17142585?hl=en) still says: "For now, this feature isn't available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account." So the official sources disagree, and some Workspace users may not see the option yet.

This matters for agencies and small businesses that run their profiles from a company Google account rather than a personal Gmail. Here is what the Gemini connection can do, who can use it today, and how to test it before the holiday rush.

## What the Gemini Business Profile connection can do

The help page groups the features into three areas. You chat with Gemini in plain language, and it makes the change or pulls the data for you.

| Area | What Gemini can do |
|---|---|
| Profile updates | Change regular and special hours, contact info, website and social links, action links for ordering, reservations and appointments, attributes, photos and location details. Food businesses can manage menus. |
| Customer engagement | View ratings and reviews, summarize feedback, draft or delete review replies, and create, schedule, publish or delete Update, Offer and Event posts. |
| Insights | Review impressions, website clicks, direction requests and bookings, plus the search keywords people used to find you. |

The help page also describes an optional business notebook for eligible owners. It needs a connected profile and your website URL, and offers proactive insights, suggested prompts and recent chats.

## Who can use it right now

The current help page lists these requirements and limits:

- You must own or manage only one verified Business Profile. Owners with access to more than one verified profile cannot use it.
- You must be 18 or older and keep Gemini Activity turned on.
- It works only in the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com, wherever that app is supported.
- The help page still excludes work and school accounts, despite the bulletin.

Search Engine Journal adds that, per the bulletin, a Workspace admin must first turn on Gemini app access in the Admin console. It also noted that the help page dropped an earlier exclusion for the EEA and UK and now says the feature is available wherever the Gemini web app is supported.

So the one profile limit is the biggest barrier for agencies and multi location brands. Workspace access does not change that.

## Other Business Profile changes in the same bulletin

Search Engine Journal reported several other updates from the bulletin. These are smaller but worth knowing:

- **Generate hours:** describe your schedule in plain words and Google fills in the hours for you to review and edit. It is limited to select regions and languages.
- **Hide ordering services:** restaurants can hide ordering providers. A service is hidden only when it is turned off for both pickup and delivery.
- **Menu tags:** menus can add Dairy-Free and Gluten-Free tags. Kosher and Halal labels should be used only where certified.
- **Appeals:** appeals now allow optional evidence uploads, and each appeal can be submitted only once.

The same bulletin moved the Maps Message button next to Call, which we covered in [Google Maps Message button moves next to Call](/google-maps-message-button-business-profile/).

## How to connect Gemini to your Business Profile

These are the steps from Google's help page:

1. Go to gemini.google.com and sign in with the account you use for your Business Profile.
2. Ask Gemini a question about your profile. If it does not use the Business Profile app, add "@Google Business Profile" to your prompt.
3. When asked, connect the app and follow the on screen steps.
4. You can disconnect it at any time in Connected Apps settings.

If you use a Workspace account and the option does not appear, check with your admin that Gemini app access is turned on. If it still does not appear, the help page limit may still apply to you.

## What local businesses should do now

These are Brandella's recommendations, not official Google guidance.

1. **Test access this week.** Try the steps above with the account that owns your profile. Note whether it works, so you know which tools your team can use before holiday hours change.
2. **Ask Gemini for your search keywords.** The insights view shows the terms people used to find you. Compare them with your categories and services, and fill any gaps.
3. **Set holiday hours early.** Use Gemini or the normal editor to add special hours for every holiday you will close or open late. Wrong hours are one of the fastest ways to lose a local customer.
4. **Review every AI change.** Gemini edits your public listing. Check hours, phone numbers and links after each change, and keep them matching your website and citations, as explained in our guide to [NAP consistency](/nap-consistency-hidden-local-seo-ranking-factor/).
5. **Draft replies, then edit.** Let Gemini summarize reviews and draft replies, but rewrite them in your own voice before posting. Generic replies help nobody.
6. **Plan for multiple locations.** If you manage more than one verified profile, Gemini will not connect, so keep your current workflow. Our [Google Business Profile guide](/google-my-business-profile-complete-guide-beginners/) and tips to [rank higher on Google Maps](/how-to-rank-higher-on-google-maps-2026/) still apply.

## What to watch next

The main open question is when the help page will match the bulletin on work accounts. Watch for that change, for support of more than one profile, and for the Generate hours feature reaching more regions. Until then, treat Workspace access as rolling out and confirm it yourself before you build a process around it.
