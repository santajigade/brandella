ChatGPT visual ads have started appearing for some users while ChatGPT creates an image. [Dataconomy reported on November 11, 2026](https://dataconomy.com/2026/11/11/chatgpt-begins-showing-ads-during-image-generation/) that users now see a banner reading "Here's an ad while we create your image," followed by images from a single advertiser that disappear once the generated picture is ready.

OpenAI first announced the format in early October in its post on a [new ChatGPT ads format and measurement partners](https://openai.com/index/new-chatgpt-ads-format-and-measurement/). At the time, [BleepingComputer reported](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-will-show-visual-ads-in-chatgpt-while-you-generate-images/) that the test would start later in October in the US with an initial group of advertisers.

This is the first ChatGPT ad placement built around a waiting moment rather than an answer. For brands with strong visuals, such as fashion, home, travel and food, it opens a new kind of inventory. For everyone else, it is a sign of how fast OpenAI is adding formats.

![ChatGPT visual ads at a glance: key numbers from OpenAI and press reports](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-visual-ads-image-generation-1.webp)

## How ChatGPT visual ads work

According to Dataconomy, the ad appears while the image is rendering and goes away when generation finishes. A user may see several images from one advertiser during that wait, and selecting the ad opens the advertiser's link.

OpenAI told BleepingComputer that "Ads will be clearly labeled, and remain separate from the image being created." OpenAI also repeated that ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. BleepingComputer says the images can show product inspiration, how a product is used, or the experience behind a service.

## Who sees the ads and who does not

| Plan | Sees image generation ads? |
|---|---|
| Free | May see them |
| Go | May see them |
| Plus, Pro | No, ad free |
| Business, Enterprise, Edu | No, ad free |

Source: Dataconomy, November 11, 2026. Dataconomy also reports that OpenAI says ads do not appear near sensitive subjects, including health and politics. It noted one counter example: Marketplace Pulse founder Juozas Kaziukėnas said he saw a pet food ad in a conversation unrelated to pets.

## How big ChatGPT Ads has become

The scale numbers come from OpenAI statements reported by the press. BleepingComputer cites OpenAI saying ChatGPT reaches 1.2 billion people weekly. Dataconomy reports that OpenAI said ChatGPT Ads now runs in 89 countries with more than 100,000 advertisers, and that more than 50 ad technology, measurement and agency partners have been added.

Dataconomy also quotes Benji Shomair, OpenAI's VP of monetization, saying at the LUMA Partners Digital Media Summit on November 9 that the company's mission "actually requires [us] to build an ad business." Note that the visual ad test itself was described as US only when it was announced.

## Measurement and brand safety partners

The October announcement also added measurement tools. BleepingComputer lists Hightouch, Tealium and LiveRamp for conversion data, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Branch, Triple Whale and Kochava among the attribution partners, and DoubleVerify and Integral Ad Science for brand suitability.

OpenAI says independent partners checking its safeguards do not get access to private user conversations. Separately, Dataconomy reports a multi image ad format entered open beta in Ads Manager on October 26, using four to six square images.

## What advertisers should do now about ChatGPT visual ads

![ChatGPT visual ads checklist: five steps for advertisers](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-visual-ads-image-generation-2.webp)

These steps are Brandella's recommendations, not official OpenAI instructions.

1. **Prepare square creative.** The multi image format uses four to six square images. Build a set that shows the product in use, not just a packshot.
2. **Match the moment.** People generating images are in a creative mood. Lifestyle and inspiration visuals will likely fit better than price led banners. Test this rather than assume it.
3. **Set up measurement first.** If you use one of the listed partners, connect it before spending, so results are comparable with your other channels.
4. **Start small and compare.** Treat it as a test budget, then compare cost and conversion rate against Meta and Google display. Our post on [ChatGPT Ads target CPA and target CPC](/chatgpt-ads-target-cpa-target-cpc/) covers the bidding options.
5. **Claim your business profile too.** Paid placement and organic presence work together. Our guide to [Businesses in ChatGPT](/businesses-in-chatgpt-business-hub-agents/) explains how to claim a profile.

## Should you move budget yet

Not much. Visual ads during image generation are new, reach only Free and Go users, and OpenAI has not shared performance data for the format. For most brands a small test is enough.

There is also a context question. Someone generating a birthday card or a logo idea did not ask for product suggestions, and the pet food example above shows targeting is still being tuned. Brands in sensitive or regulated categories should watch where their ads appear before scaling, and use the brand suitability partners if they can.

On the upside, the waiting time is short and the ad sits next to a visual task, so it suits products that people judge by looks. Fashion, furniture, travel, beauty and food brands have the most obvious fit. B2B software and services will likely get more from text placements next to answers.
