AI in PPC is no longer a feature you switch on. In 2026 it is the way Google Ads works. Bids, search terms, ad text, landing pages and even the places your ads appear inside AI answers are now chosen by machine learning, often by default.

So should advertisers be worried? A little, and for the right reasons. This guide, first published in November 2025 and fully refreshed for 2026, shows what the AI now decides, what you still control, where the real risks are and a practical checklist to stay in charge.

## What AI in PPC means in 2026

When people say AI in PPC they usually mean four systems working together inside Google Ads. Each one takes over a decision that a human used to make by hand.

| System | What the AI decides | What you give it |
|---|---|---|
| Smart Bidding | The bid for every single auction | A goal (CPA or ROAS) and accurate conversions |
| AI Max for Search | Extra search terms, ad text and landing pages | Keywords, assets, website, brand and URL rules |
| Performance Max | Channels, placements, audiences and creative mix | Asset groups, audience signals, a product feed |
| Ads in AI Overviews and AI Mode | When your ad appears inside an AI answer | Nothing extra: existing campaigns qualify |

The common thread is simple. You supply the inputs and the guardrails, and the system makes thousands of small choices you never see one by one.

## The Google Ads AI features you need to know

### Smart Bidding sets a bid for every auction

Google describes [automated bidding](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2979071) as bidding based on how likely each ad is to bring a click or conversion. Smart Bidding is the conversion based group of strategies: Target CPA, Target ROAS, Maximize conversions and Maximize conversion value.

It uses auction time bidding, which sets a bid for every query using signals such as device, location, time of day, remarketing list, language and operating system. The same page notes that Enhanced CPC stopped being available for Search and Display campaigns from the week of March 31, 2025, so manual bidding with a light AI assist is gone.

### AI Max for Search campaigns

AI Max is the biggest change to Search in years. Google's help page on [how AI Max for Search campaigns works](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15910187?hl=en) says it is not a new campaign type. It is a suite of features you turn on inside an existing Search campaign:

- **Search term matching** expands your keywords with broad match and keywordless technology. You can turn it off per ad group.
- **Text customization** writes new headlines and descriptions from your ads, landing pages and assets using generative AI.
- **Final URL expansion** sends a click to the page on your site that Google thinks fits the query best. It needs text customization to be on.

Google says advertisers that turn on AI Max typically see 14 percent more conversions or conversion value at a similar CPA or ROAS, based on its own 2025 data for non retail advertisers. Treat that as Google's average, not a promise for your account.

AI Max is also becoming the default. The [Google Ads Editor 2.14 release notes](https://support.google.com/google-ads/editor/answer/18835571) say AI Max, text asset automation and final URL expansion are now on by default when you create a new Search campaign in Editor. We covered that release in our post on [Google Ads Editor 2.14 and the AI Max default](/google-ads-editor-2-14-ai-max-retention/).

### Performance Max is less of a black box

Performance Max still hands most decisions to Google, but the reporting gap that made advertisers nervous in 2025 has narrowed. Google's update on [more visibility and control in Performance Max](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16451273) lists:

- Search terms reporting for all Performance Max campaigns and through the API
- Campaign level negative keywords for Search and Shopping inventory, plus shared negative keyword lists
- A channel performance report, launched as a beta
- Full asset stats, and final URL expansion pages you can now see and remove
- Device and age controls, plus a beta for gender controls
- Up to 50 search themes per asset group, up from 25

Google's [Performance Max steering and reporting updates for 2026](https://business.google.com/aunz/accelerate/resources/articles/new-performance-max-steering-and-reporting-updates-coming-in-2026/) add customer list exclusions (so you can stop paying to reach people who already bought), a budget report that projects end of month spend, audience reporting by age and gender, and placement reports split by network. If you run PMax, our [step by step Performance Max optimization guide](/how-to-optimize-performance-max-campaign-step-by-step/) shows where each control sits.

### Ads inside AI Overviews and AI Mode

This is the newest part of AI in PPC, and the one with the least control. Google's help page on [ads in AI powered experiences in Search](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/18826097?hl=en) says existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns are automatically eligible to show ads within AI Overviews and AI Mode. No new campaign or budget is needed.

The details matter:

- AI Max, broad match, Shopping and Performance Max can match to both the query and the AI response. Exact and phrase match keywords only qualify when the query shows direct commercial intent.
- You cannot target AI Overviews or AI Mode on their own, or opt out of them separately from Search. Negative keywords and brand exclusions still apply.
- Results are folded into your normal campaign reports. There is no separate AI Overviews report.
- Ads are labelled "Sponsored". Google may show short AI explainers next to them, and advertisers cannot write, edit or opt out of these.
- Ads inside AI answers run in English in the United States and a list of other English speaking markets, and can appear across follow up questions in AI Mode.

For the latest market list, see our news post on [AI Mode ads expanding to 10 countries](/google-ai-mode-ads-multiturn-expansion/).

## What advertisers still control

The fear that "the machine does everything" is not quite true. The controls have moved from the keyword and bid level to the input and guardrail level. Here is where your influence now lives.

| You control | Why it matters more with AI |
|---|---|
| Conversion goals and values | Smart Bidding chases exactly what you count. Count junk leads and it finds more junk leads. |
| Budget | Google warns that AI Max will not be effective if a campaign is limited by budget. |
| Negative keywords | The main way to keep broad, keywordless and AI answer matching away from bad queries. |
| Brand inclusions and exclusions | Decide whether AI Max can chase your own brand, or competitors' brands. |
| URL inclusions and exclusions | Stop final URL expansion sending clicks to careers, blog or support pages. |
| Locations of interest | AI Max can reach people by the place they search about, not only where they are. |
| Assets and text automation | Remove generated assets you do not like, or turn text customization off. |
| Customer list exclusions | Keep Performance Max focused on new customers instead of past buyers. |

One trap is worth knowing. Google's AI Max help page says pinned responsive search ad assets are not respected when final URL expansion is on. If legal or brand rules need a fixed headline, test carefully before switching it on.

## Should advertisers be worried? A realistic answer

Worry depends on what kind of account you run. This is our own assessment based on how the features above behave.

| Advertiser type | Worry level | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Regulated industries (finance, health, legal) | High | Generated text and dynamic landing pages can break approved wording. Review every asset. |
| Lead generation with weak lead quality tracking | High | AI optimizes to form fills, not to customers, unless you import offline conversions. |
| Small budgets, few conversions | Medium | The AI has little data to learn from, and budget limits blunt AI Max. |
| Ecommerce with a clean feed and values | Low | Value based bidding, PMax and Shopping ads in AI answers suit this setup well. |
| Brand heavy accounts | Medium | Broad and keywordless matching can drift into brand or competitor terms without rules. |

The real risks are not robots taking jobs. They are quieter: paying for queries you would never have chosen, sending clicks to the wrong page, ads that say something off brand, and less clean data on where AI answer ads perform. Every one of these has a control. The danger is not using it.

## An AI in PPC checklist for 2026

![AI in PPC checklist for 2026: seven steps to stay in control of Google Ads AI](/images/infographics/ig-ai-in-ppc-should-advertisers-be-worried-1.webp)

Run through this before you switch on AI Max or launch a new Performance Max campaign:

1. **Fix measurement first.** Check every primary conversion action, remove soft goals like page views, and add values. Our guide to [value based bidding in Google Ads](/value-based-bidding-google-ads-explained/) explains how.
2. **Pick the right bid goal.** Use Target CPA or Target ROAS once you have steady conversion volume; use Maximize conversions while you build it.
3. **Set guardrails before launch.** Add account and campaign negative keywords, brand exclusions and URL exclusions on day one, not after the first bad week.
4. **Check your landing pages.** Final URL expansion can pick any relevant page, so make sure weak or off topic pages are excluded and dynamic pages load correctly.
5. **Review generated text.** Read the text customization assets and remove anything inaccurate.
6. **Give the budget room.** Do not launch AI Max in a campaign that is already limited by budget.
7. **Compare against a baseline.** Note last month's CPA, ROAS and conversion rate, then judge AI changes after enough conversions, not after two days.

## A 30 minute weekly AI PPC review

Automation does not remove work; it changes it. This short weekly routine catches most problems early.

- **Search terms (10 minutes).** Filter the search terms report for the "AI Max" match type, and the Performance Max search terms view. Add negatives for anything irrelevant. Our post on the [search term matchtype ValueTrack parameter](/search-term-matchtype-valuetrack-ai-max/) shows how to split AI Max clicks in your own analytics too.
- **Landing pages (5 minutes).** In the landing pages report, the "Selected by" column shows pages AI Max chose. Exclude any that convert badly.
- **Assets (5 minutes).** Remove generated headlines or images that are off brand or untrue.
- **Channels and placements (5 minutes).** In PMax, check the channel report and placements for spend going somewhere unexpected.
- **Conversions (5 minutes).** Look for sudden jumps in conversions with no jump in sales. That usually means tracking broke, not that the AI got smarter.

## How the PPC specialist's job is changing

The manual tasks of 2020, such as adjusting bids by hand or building hundreds of exact match ad groups, are mostly handled by the system now. That does not make specialists less valuable. It moves the value to the work AI cannot do from inside the ad account.

That work includes knowing the real profit margin of each product, feeding back which leads became customers, writing a sharp offer the AI can remix, choosing which pages deserve traffic and spotting when a metric looks too good to be true. Strategy, data quality and judgement are the job now.

## FAQ about AI in PPC

### Is AI going to replace PPC specialists?

No. AI has taken over bid setting, query matching and asset mixing, but it still needs a person to define goals, supply clean conversion data, set guardrails and judge results against real business profit.

### Is AI Max the same as Performance Max?

No. Google says AI Max is not a new campaign type. It is a set of features inside a Search campaign, so you keep keywords, ad groups and Search reporting. Performance Max is a separate campaign type that runs across all Google channels.

### Can I stop my ads showing in AI Overviews or AI Mode?

Not separately. Google says you cannot opt out of AI Overviews or AI Mode on their own while still running on Search. Your negative keywords and brand exclusions still apply there.

### What is the biggest mistake advertisers make with AI in PPC?

Feeding the AI the wrong goal. If your conversions include low value actions or spam leads, Smart Bidding and AI Max will find more of them very efficiently. Fix conversion tracking before you expand automation.

## Stay curious, not worried

AI in PPC is here to stay, and it will keep taking over more of the mechanics. Advertisers who do well in 2026 are not the ones who resist it or hand it everything. They are the ones who set clear goals, keep their guardrails tight and spend the time they save on strategy, offers and data quality.

Start with the checklist above, run the 30 minute review every week and judge every AI feature by one question: is it bringing customers who are worth what you pay for them?
