YouTube gambling ads can now appear on two of YouTube's most valuable surfaces, the Home feed and Search results. Google posted the change in its [Advertising Policies Help notice](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/18951667?hl=en) on November 19, 2026, and it took effect the same day.

Three groups gain access: certified online gambling advertisers, certified fantasy sports advertisers and prediction market advertisers. Everyone else keeps the old rules. If you run campaigns for a betting brand, a sportsbook affiliate or a fantasy app, the inventory you can buy on YouTube just got bigger.

Google's wording is direct: "On November 19, 2026, YouTube's ad requirements will be updated to permit advertising on Home and Search for the following categories where permitted by the Google Ads policies and local law."

![YouTube gambling ads on Home and Search: who qualifies after November 19, 2026](/images/infographics/ig-youtube-gambling-ads-home-search-1.webp)

## Who can run YouTube gambling ads on Home and Search

The notice lists three categories, each with its own condition.

| Category | Condition in Google's notice |
| --- | --- |
| Online gambling | Certified online gambling advertisers who meet the Gambling and games policy requirements. Advertisers of online gambling promoting content that is not subject to mandatory state or national gambling registration are excluded |
| Online non-casino games (fantasy sports) | Certified fantasy sports advertisers who meet the Gambling and games policy requirements and target the United States or Canada |
| Prediction markets | A Prediction Markets certification to serve ads in the USA, or an online gambling certification in other regions |

All three still sit under the [Gambling and games policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15132179), so country lists and licence rules there still apply. Home and Search access does not replace certification. You need the certification first.

## What changed compared with before

YouTube keeps separate ad requirements for its feed surfaces on top of the normal Google Ads policies. Google rewrote those requirements in its [YouTube and Discover Feed ad requirements update](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17455694) in August 2026, and gambling was one of the restricted categories.

In October, Google published a separate [YouTube Masthead notice](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/18429295) that let certified online gambling advertisers use the Masthead. [PPC Land reported](https://ppc.land/gambling-ads-reach-youtube-home-and-search-29-days-after-the-masthead/) that the Home and Search change came 29 days after that Masthead update.

| Surface | Before | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube Masthead | Opened to certified online gambling advertisers in October 2026 | Unchanged |
| YouTube Home | Not allowed for these categories | Allowed for the three certified groups |
| YouTube Search | Not allowed for these categories | Allowed for the three certified groups |

The notice was posted and applied on the same date, so advertisers got no lead time. If your team plans YouTube budgets weeks ahead, this inventory was not in those plans.

## Why the prediction markets line matters

Prediction markets get their own line, and the rule changes by country. In the US, Google asks for a [Prediction Markets certification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16757872). Outside the US, the same advertiser needs an online gambling certification.

So a prediction market brand that runs in several countries may need two different certifications to reach Home and Search everywhere. Check which one covers each market before you widen targeting.

## What gambling and gaming advertisers should do now

![What to check before running YouTube gambling ads on Home and Search](/images/infographics/ig-youtube-gambling-ads-home-search-2.webp)

These are our recommendations, based on Google's notice. They are not official Google guidance.

1. Confirm your certification. Open the certification status in your Google Ads account and make sure it covers every country you target. Home and Search access depends on it.
2. Check country targeting for fantasy sports. Fantasy sports access applies only when you target the United States or Canada. A campaign that also targets other countries may not qualify there.
3. Pick the right prediction markets certification. Use the Prediction Markets certification for the US and an online gambling certification elsewhere.
4. Review video creative for feed and search placements. Home and Search ads compete with organic videos. Make the first frames and thumbnails clear about what you offer and who it is for, within your licence terms.
5. Watch placement reports for two weeks. Compare views, cost and conversions before and after November 19 so you can see what Home and Search add.
6. Re-read local law. Google's notice says the change applies "where permitted by the Google Ads policies and local law." A Google approval does not cover local advertising rules.

## FAQ about YouTube gambling ads

### When did YouTube gambling ads become allowed on Home and Search?

On November 19, 2026. Google posted the notice that day and said the change took effect on the same date.

### Do I need a certification to run these ads?

Yes. Online gambling and fantasy sports advertisers must be certified and meet the Gambling and games policy. Prediction markets need a Prediction Markets certification in the US or an online gambling certification in other regions.

### Can fantasy sports ads run on YouTube Home in any country?

No. The notice says certified fantasy sports advertisers qualify when they target the United States or Canada.

### Does this change the YouTube Masthead rules?

No. The Masthead opened to certified online gambling advertisers in a separate October 2026 update. This notice covers Home and Search.
