ChatGPT external links are showing up far more often. New data from Peec AI, a Berlin based AI search analytics company, suggests that the share of ChatGPT conversations with at least one clickable link to an outside website has jumped from about 10% to nearly half.

The figures come from a [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7528032942187663360/) published on November 16, 2026 by David Konitzny, a GEO researcher at Peec AI, and were reported by [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chatgpt-links-to-websites-in-nearly-half-of-chats-up-from-10-peec-ai-finds/) the same day. On November 14, 47.8% of tracked chats contained an external link.

For publishers, brands and SEO teams, this is one of the clearest signs yet that ChatGPT is becoming a traffic source and not just an answer box. But Peec AI itself warns that links are not the same as clicks.

![Infographic with key numbers from the Peec AI study on ChatGPT external links](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-external-links-half-of-chats-1.webp)

## What the Peec AI data shows

Peec AI tracked daily conversations from August 27 to November 14, 2026. It counted an external link only when ChatGPT placed a clickable link to an outside website within the answer. Citations and links that open a sidebar were excluded.

Here are the main numbers, as reported by PPC Land from Konitzny's charts.

| Measure | Before October | Mid November |
|---|---|---|
| Chats with at least one external link | About 10% | 47.8% on November 14 |
| Peak share | Not applicable | 49.5% on October 14 |
| Average links per linked chat | Roughly 2 | Roughly 4 |
| Linked chats with only one link | About 43% | 18% |

The jump was sudden. The share reached 41% on October 9. The chart marks October 7 as the day GPT-6 became ChatGPT's default model, though the post does not prove that the model change caused the rise.

## Links are not the same as citations

One finding stands out for SEO teams. According to the study, 63% of linked URLs were not among the cited sources in the same chat, and 47% of linked domains were not among the cited domains.

In other words, the site ChatGPT cites as a source and the site it links to for the reader can be different. Being cited does not guarantee the click, and a site can earn a link without being the main source. ## The limits of this ChatGPT external links study

Peec AI has not disclosed the sample size, prompt set, market, language, ChatGPT plan or whether users were logged in. The figures have not been independently replicated, as PPC Land notes.

Konitzny also added an important caveat in the post: "more external links don't automatically mean more clicks or referral traffic." The study contains no click or traffic data. Treat it as a strong signal of how ChatGPT answers are changing, not as proof that your traffic will rise. Peec AI has studied ChatGPT behaviour before, for example in its research showing that [ChatGPT often searches in English](https://peec.ai/blog/chatgpt-searches-in-english-even-when-you-don-t) even when users ask in other languages.

## What brands and SEO teams should do now

These are Brandella's recommendations based on the data above, not guidance from OpenAI or Peec AI.

![Checklist of six steps to earn more clicks from ChatGPT external links](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-external-links-half-of-chats-2.webp)

1. **Check your ChatGPT referrals.** In GA4, look at sessions from chatgpt.com in your traffic sources report and compare September with November.
2. **Group AI traffic.** Build a custom channel group for AI assistants in GA4 so visits from ChatGPT and similar tools are easy to track over time.
3. **Make pages worth linking.** ChatGPT now often gives several links per answer. Pages with clear answers, original data and useful tools stand out in a list of four.
4. **Fix landing pages.** If AI visitors land on deep pages, make sure those pages load fast, explain themselves and offer a clear next step.
5. **Test your key prompts.** Ask ChatGPT the questions your customers ask and note which sites get linked, not just cited.
6. **Do not chase volume alone.** More links may mean less attention per link, so measure engagement and conversions from AI visits, not just sessions.

## Why this matters for AI search strategy

For two years, many publishers worried that AI answers would keep users inside the chat. These numbers suggest ChatGPT is now sending readers outward far more often, at least in the conversations Peec AI tracks.

That raises the value of being a site AI assistants want to link to. ## FAQ about ChatGPT external links

### How often does ChatGPT link to websites now?

Peec AI data shows 47.8% of tracked chats had at least one external link on November 14, 2026, up from about 10% before October.

### Does this mean more traffic from ChatGPT?

Not necessarily. Peec AI says more external links don't automatically mean more clicks or referral traffic, and the study has no click data.

### Did GPT-6 cause the change?

The chart marks October 7 as the day GPT-6 became the default model, just before the jump. The study does not prove the model change caused it.

### Are links the same as citations in ChatGPT?

No. The study found 63% of linked URLs were not among the same chat's cited sources.
