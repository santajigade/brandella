Google has preannounced the Google December 2026 core update. On November 24, 2026, Google posted a [Search Status Dashboard entry](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/AXrqxXXdtSjj3672ux7X) at 07:03 PST that says "This update is scheduled to be released in December 2026." The update is not live yet, and Google has not given a start date or said how long the rollout will take.

The notice came on the same day the November 2026 core update finished. That leaves site owners with a short window, possibly only a few weeks, between two broad updates. It also means any analysis of the November update has to be wrapped up quickly.

Google says the update applies to all locations and languages where Search is available. The notice landed on the same day Google launched its new [Search ranking hub](/google-search-ranking-hub/), which describes how it gives advance notice of updates.

![December 2026 core update facts: preannounced November 24, 2026, scheduled for December 2026, all locations and languages, November update ran about 14 days](/images/infographics/ig-google-december-2026-core-update-1.webp)

## What the Google December 2026 core update targets

Google's dashboard entry describes the update as focusing on "multiple improvements to how our systems understand and rank relevant content (including for longer and more complex questions), as well as improving personalized results for people who have personalization turned on."

So pages that answer detailed, multi part questions are in scope, which fits how people now phrase conversational searches. The personalization change affects people who have personalization turned on, and Google has not said how much results will vary from person to person.

Google adds that "our guidance about general core updates applies," so there is no special fix for this one. Google has not published a Search Central blog post about it, and the dashboard entry is the only official source so far. Expect the start date to appear there first.

## Why Google is announcing it early

Advance notice is now part of Google's stated process. Its new [stay informed page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/ranking/stay-informed) says Google gives notice of "significant ranking changes" such as core updates, and that UK notice follows the Competition and Markets Authority's fair ranking conduct requirements for Google.

The same page names the Search Status Dashboard as the main record, with an Atom feed for alerts. If you only watch social posts for update news, add the feed so your team sees the start date the moment Google posts it.

## The November 2026 core update has finished

The [November 2026 core update](/google-november-2026-core-update/) started on November 10 and, according to the [dashboard completion entry](https://status.search.google.com/incidents/WLxcJyC6jMWMfryxsKMc), was complete as of November 24, 2026. [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-november-2026-core-update-done-42571.html) put the completion at 9:03 am ET and the duration at 14 days.

Here is how 2026's broad core updates compare, using dates reported by [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-december-2026-core-update/598760/) and the dashboard:

| Core update | Rollout |
|---|---|
| March 2026 | March 27 to April 8 |
| May 2026 | May 21 to June 2 |
| November 2026 | November 10 to November 24 |
| December 2026 | Preannounced November 24, due in December |

Rank data from mid November needs care. A Google sign-in wall test cut results reaching some rank trackers on November 18, as we covered in our report on [rank tracker data gaps](/google-sign-in-wall-rank-trackers/).

## What site owners should do before December

![Five steps to prepare for the December 2026 core update](/images/infographics/ig-google-december-2026-core-update-2.webp)

These are Brandella recommendations based on Google's notice and its ranking pages:

1. Close your November analysis. Google advises waiting until an update is complete "plus at least a week of post-rollout data." Compare the week from December 1 with a week before November 10, if December has not started by then.
2. Mark both updates in your reports: add November 10, November 24 and the December start date (once posted) as annotations in your analytics and rank tracking tools.
3. Review pages that answer complex questions, and check whether your guides fully answer the detailed, multi part questions people ask, not just the short head term.
4. Expect more varied rankings. With personalization in scope, a rank tracker may show one position while real users see others. Lean on Search Console data over single position checks.
5. Send feedback if it is warranted. Google's [feedback page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/ranking/submit-feedback) says verified owners can use Submit Feedback in Search Console for dashboard listed updates. It will not change rankings for single URLs.

## FAQ about the December 2026 core update

### When does the December 2026 core update start?

Google has only said it is scheduled for release in December 2026. No exact date or rollout length has been announced.

### Is the December 2026 core update live now?

No. It was preannounced on November 24, 2026. Watch the Search Status Dashboard for the start entry.

### What does the update focus on?

Google says it improves how its systems understand and rank relevant content, including for longer and more complex questions, and improves personalized results for people with personalization on.

### Should I make changes before it rolls out?

Google says its general core update guidance applies, so there is no specific fix. Focus on helpful content and finish your review of the November update first.
