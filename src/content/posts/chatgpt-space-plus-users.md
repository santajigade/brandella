ChatGPT Space is now reaching the $20 Plus plan. OpenAI's [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) for November 20, 2026 say "Starting today, ChatGPT Space is rolling out globally to Plus users ages 18 and older," as quoted by [ShiftDelete.Net](https://shiftdelete.net/en/chatgpt-space-plus-rollout).

Until now, Space was limited to Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts. [Data Studios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/chatgpt-space-rolls-out-to-plus-users-replaces-library-pages-slides) reports that it launched for those plans on September 29 and that ChatGPT Slides arrived for them on October 16.

Plus is the plan many freelancers, consultants and small agency teams already pay for. That puts shared AI pages and decks within reach of people who build client reports and content briefs every week.

## What ChatGPT Space is

OpenAI's help article on [Space sharing, data and controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001544-chatgpt-space-sharing-data-and-controls) puts it simply: "Space is the home for your files and pages in ChatGPT. It replaces Library, while your Projects stay as they are."

Pages are the core unit. OpenAI says "Pages are editable documents that you can organize inside other pages and work on with other people." Each collaborator works on shared content with their own ChatGPT agent.

According to Data Studios and ShiftDelete, Space holds pages, slides, images and uploaded files, and pages can mix text, images, charts and interactive elements. Both report that pages can be set to stay updated from connected apps and tools. Data Studios gives Slack, Google Drive, email and calendar as examples.

## ChatGPT Space plans and limits

| Item | What has been reported |
|---|---|
| Plus | Rolling out globally from November 20, ages 18 and older |
| Pro, Business, Enterprise | Already had Space since September |
| Free and Go | Not included, and cannot open shared pages or decks |
| Public links | Not offered |
| Slides on the web | Export to PPTX, PDF and Google Slides, per Data Studios |
| Storage and collaborator limits | Not documented, per Data Studios |

Reports differ on mobile. ShiftDelete says pages can be created and edited in the iOS and Android apps, while Data Studios lists mobile as mainly for finding, reading and sharing pages. Check the app on your own account before you plan around it.

"Rolling out" also means not every Plus account has Space yet. No completion date has been given.

## How sharing and privacy work in ChatGPT Space

Pages often hold client data, so the sharing rules matter.

You choose who can see a page and whether they can view or edit it. Owners can review who has access, change permissions, remove collaborators and revoke access. In managed workspaces, admin sharing controls also apply.

Sharing a page does not give anyone your private chats or your Memory. But if Memory is on, ChatGPT may use personal context while writing a page, and once that text is on the page, everyone with access can read it. OpenAI's own example is a project brief that draws on an earlier conversation.

Files work the same way. A file uploaded into a page follows that page's permissions, while a link to a file stored elsewhere does not grant access to open it. Anything copied or summarized onto the page from a connected service is visible to viewers.

On training, OpenAI says use follows the setting of the person using the agent, found under Settings, Data controls, "Improve the model for everyone". When a colleague's agent works on your shared page, their setting applies, not yours. OpenAI adds: "OpenAI does not train on business and enterprise data by default."

## What to do now

![What marketers should do now with ChatGPT Space](/images/infographics/ig-chatgpt-space-plus-users-2.webp)

These are Brandella's recommendations.

1. Check whether Space has replaced Library in your sidebar. If not, the rollout has not reached your account yet.
2. Before you share any page with a client, turn Memory off for that work or read the page line by line. Memory can pull in details from unrelated chats.
3. Review your training setting under Data controls, and remember each collaborator's own setting applies to their agent.
4. Do not paste client credentials or raw customer data into shared pages. Link to the source file instead, since a link does not grant access.
5. Confirm your client can open the page. Free and Go accounts cannot, and there are no public links, so export slides to PPTX or PDF for those clients.
6. Set a naming system for pages, since pages can sit inside other pages and grow quickly.
7. If you plan to sell AI help to clients, read our guide on [GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](/gpt-6-2-astra-sol-marketers/) to see which models sit behind this work.

## FAQ about ChatGPT Space

### Who can use ChatGPT Space now?

Plus users ages 18 and older as the rollout reaches them, plus Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts, which already had it.

### Can free ChatGPT users see a page I share?

No. Reports say opening a shared page or presentation needs view permission and a Plus, Pro, Business or Enterprise account with Space access.

### Does sharing a page expose my chat history?

No. OpenAI says sharing does not give access to your private chats or Memory. Text that ChatGPT writes onto the page from that context is visible, though.

### What happened to my Library?

OpenAI says Space replaces Library as the home for your files and pages. Projects are not changed.
