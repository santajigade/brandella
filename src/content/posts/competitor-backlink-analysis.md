<p class="bx-lede">Competitor backlink analysis is how you stop guessing why a rival outranks you. The page sitting at position one on Google carries roughly 3.8 times more backlinks than pages ranked two through ten, and a step-by-step gap analysis is the fastest way to find out exactly which links are missing from your own profile.</p>

<p>Most SEOs know backlinks matter. Fewer actually run a structured process to find which specific domains link to competitors but not to them, which is the difference between guessing at outreach targets and working from a warm, pre-qualified list.</p>

<p>The median page-one website carries 907 referring domains. That number alone explains why content quality by itself rarely closes a ranking gap against an established competitor.</p>

<p>This guide walks through the full seven-step process, from identifying true competitors to building a qualified outreach list, plus the tools and filters that separate a winnable prospect from a dead end.</p>

<h2 id="what-is-it"><span class="bx-num">01</span>What Competitor Backlink Analysis Actually Involves</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>Competitor backlink analysis means running a rival's domain through a backlink checker, then reviewing referring domain count, authority scores, anchor text patterns, and link types to find opportunities you can realistically replicate. <a href="https://ahrefs.com/blog/backlink-gap-analysis" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Ahrefs' own backlink gap analysis guide</a> frames this as the process of finding great links your competitors have earned that you haven't yet.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.icopify.com/seo-growth/competitor-backlinks-analysis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Icopify's 2026 seven-step guide</a> adds an important nuance for where the practice is headed: it's no longer just "how do I outrank them on Google." With AI-driven search traffic growing fast through 2025 and 2026, competitor analysis increasingly needs a second lens covering the trust signals that shape AI-generated answers too, not only classic blue-link rankings.</p>

<h2 id="step-1-competitors"><span class="bx-num">02</span>Step 1: Identify Your True SEO Competitors</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>Start in your backlink tool's Site Explorer or equivalent, enter your domain, and pull the Organic Competitors or Competing Domains report. This surfaces sites ranking for the same keywords as you, which is a more useful starting list than your actual business rivals.</p>

<p><a href="https://jeenaminfotech.com/blog/competitor-backlink-analysis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Jeenaminfotech's 2026 guide to the process</a> recommends checking authority scores at this stage too, confirming each candidate is genuinely relevant to your niche before spending time pulling its full backlink profile.</p>

<div class="bx-note">
<span class="lbl">Business Competitors Aren't Always SEO Competitors</span>
<p>A company you compete with for customers may rank for entirely different keywords than you, while a content site with no product overlap might dominate the exact search terms you're targeting. Pick 3 to 5 domains based on keyword overlap, not brand rivalry.</p>
</div>

<h2 id="step-2-pull-profiles"><span class="bx-num">03</span>Step 2: Pull Each Competitor's Backlink Profile</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>With your 3 to 5 competitors set, pull each one's full referring domain list. <a href="https://www.digitalmarketingagency.sg/blog/backlink-analysis-tools" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DMA's 2026 comparison of backlink tools</a> recommends auditing your own profile first too, establishing a baseline Domain Rating and referring-domain count before you start comparing against rivals.</p>

<p>This baseline matters more than it seems. Without it, a competitor's raw backlink count means nothing, you need your own starting point to measure the actual gap.</p>

<h2 id="step-3-gap-analysis"><span class="bx-num">04</span>Step 3: Run a Backlink Gap or Link Intersect Analysis</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>This is where the process gets its teeth. <a href="https://growwithsakib.com/backlink-gap-analysis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Growwithsakib's July 2026 breakdown</a> explains the core mechanic clearly: enter your domain plus three or four competitors, and the tool returns every referring domain linking to them but not to you.</p>

<p>Sort the results by intersect count, how many of your competitors each domain links to. A domain linking to three of your four rivals has publicly demonstrated it's willing to link to a business exactly like yours, which makes it a far stronger prospect than a cold, unqualified lead. <a href="https://attrock.com/blog/backlink-gap-analysis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Attrock's guide to backlink gap analysis</a> walks through the same Link Intersect workflow inside Ahrefs' Site Explorer if you need the exact click path.</p>

<h2 id="step-4-filter-quality"><span class="bx-num">05</span>Step 4: Filter for Quality, Not Just Volume</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>A raw list of hundreds of gap domains isn't a prospect list yet, it's a starting point. <a href="https://authority.builders/blog/link-gap-analysis/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Authority Builders' 2026 guide to link gap analysis</a> lays out three practical filters worth applying before anything else.</p>

<div class="bx-checklist">
<div class="bx-check-item"><div class="bx-check-icon"></div><p><strong>Domain Rating 30 or higher.</strong> Links from sites below DR 20 rarely drive meaningful ranking or authority signals.</p></div>
<div class="bx-check-item"><div class="bx-check-icon"></div><p><strong>Verify real organic traffic.</strong> A DR 50 domain with zero visitors is often a link farm or an abandoned site.</p></div>
<div class="bx-check-item"><div class="bx-check-icon"></div><p><strong>Confirm topical relevance.</strong> A high-intersect domain still needs to make sense for your niche, not just your competitors'.</p></div>
</div>

<h2 id="step-5-winnability"><span class="bx-num">06</span>Step 5: Assess Winnability, Not Just Opportunity</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>Growwithsakib's analysis, referenced above, makes a point most gap-analysis guides skip entirely: the export is the easy part. For each domain, the real question is why it linked to your competitor and whether you can realistically replicate that reason.</p>

<p>Plenty of gaps simply can't be won, paid placements, exclusive partner deals, or a guest post the competitor personally wrote. Sorting winnable opportunities from dead ends before outreach begins saves hours of wasted emails later.</p>

function bxCalc(){
  var selects = document.querySelectorAll('.bx-calc-select');
  var total = 0;
  selects.forEach(function(s){
    var weight = parseFloat(s.getAttribute('data-weight'));
    var value = parseFloat(s.value);
    total += weight * value;
  });
  var pct = Math.round(total);
  document.getElementById('bxGaugeFill').style.width = pct + '%';
  document.getElementById('bxGaugeScore').innerText = pct + '%';
  var verdict = document.getElementById('bxVerdict');
  if(pct >= 80){ verdict.innerText = "High-priority prospect. Move this to the top of your outreach list."; }
  else if(pct >= 50){ verdict.innerText = "Worth pursuing. Refine your pitch around the specific reason it links out."; }
  else if(pct > 0){ verdict.innerText = "Low priority. Time is likely better spent on a stronger prospect."; }
  else { verdict.innerText = "Select an option for each factor to calculate your score."; }
}

<h2 id="faq"><span class="bx-num">12</span>FAQs on Competitor Backlink Analysis</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>

<div class="bx-faq" id="bxFaq">
  <button class="bx-q">How many competitors should I analyze?<span class="ic">+</span></button>
  <div class="bx-a"><p>3 to 5 domains is the practical range. More adds noise to the gap analysis without meaningfully improving the prospect list.</p></div>
  <button class="bx-q">What's a good Domain Rating threshold for backlink prospects?<span class="ic">+</span></button>
  <div class="bx-a"><p>DR 30 or higher is a common filter. Links from sites below DR 20 rarely move rankings or authority signals meaningfully.</p></div>
  <button class="bx-q">Is Ahrefs or Semrush better for competitor backlink analysis?<span class="ic">+</span></button>
  <div class="bx-a"><p>Ahrefs generally leads on backlink depth and discovery speed. Semrush's Backlink Gap tool and bundled toxic-link auditing make it stronger for ongoing workflows. Many teams use both.</p></div>
  <button class="bx-q">How often should I re-run a competitor backlink analysis?<span class="ic">+</span></button>
  <div class="bx-a"><p>Monthly. Competitor backlink profiles shift regularly, so a one-time audit goes stale within a few weeks.</p></div>
  <button class="bx-q">What makes a backlink gap "winnable"?<span class="ic">+</span></button>
  <div class="bx-a"><p>The link needs an editorial or replicable reason, like a resource page or guest post opportunity, rather than a paid placement or exclusive partnership you can't recreate.</p></div>
</div>
