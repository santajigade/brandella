<h2 id="factors"><span class="bx-num">01</span>The Three Ranking Factors, Explained</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>According to <a href="https://www.morrow-marketing.com/post/how-to-rank-in-google-maps-your-2026-local-seo-blueprint" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Morrow Marketing's 2026 local SEO blueprint</a>, Google evaluates every business on relevance (how well your listing matches the search), distance (proximity to the searcher), and prominence (how trusted and well-known you appear online).</p>

<p>These three factors don't operate independently. According to <a href="https://almcorp.com/blog/google-maps-seo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ALM Corp's complete local SEO playbook for 2026</a>, exceptional prominence can partially offset a distance disadvantage, and a fully optimized profile increases relevance across a broader range of searches.</p>

<h2 id="category"><span class="bx-num">02</span>Your Primary Category Is the #1 Signal to Rank Higher on Google Maps</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>According to <a href="https://connascent.com/blog/google-business-profile-ranking-2026" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Connascent's 2026 guide based on Whitespark's survey data</a>, your primary category is the single most important optimization decision for anyone trying to rank higher on Google Maps, outweighing reviews and links combined.</p>

<div class="bx-note">
<span class="lbl">Specificity Beats Generic Categories</span>
<p>According to ALM Corp's guide referenced above, "Emergency Plumber" outperforms "Plumber" if emergency services are genuinely your primary offering. The guiding principle is specificity, not the category with the fewest competitors.</p>
</div>

<div class="bx-check-item"><div class="bx-check-icon"></div><p><strong>Add secondary categories</strong> only where they genuinely reflect services you offer.</p></div>

<h2 id="reviews"><span class="bx-num">03</span>Review Recency Beats Review Count</h2>
<div class="bx-rule"></div>
<p>According to <a href="https://www.zentricsolutions.com/blogs/rank-business-higher-google-maps" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Zentric Solutions' 2026 guide to ranking higher on Google Maps</a>, a business with 50 reviews gained over the past year will generally outrank a business with 200 reviews that are all three years old.</p>

<p>Local pack businesses typically carry 2 to 3 times more reviews than those ranking below them, but the underlying driver is consistent, fresh review velocity, not a single historical total.</p>

function bxCalc(){
  var selects = document.querySelectorAll('.bx-calc-select');
  var total = 0;
  selects.forEach(function(s){
    var weight = parseFloat(s.getAttribute('data-weight'));
    var value = parseFloat(s.value);
    total += weight * value;
  });
  var pct = Math.round(total);
  document.getElementById('bxGaugeFill').style.width = pct + '%';
  document.getElementById('bxGaugeScore').innerText = pct + '%';
  var verdict = document.getElementById('bxVerdict');
  if(pct >= 80){ verdict.innerText = "Strong setup. Keep the review velocity and content cadence steady to maintain it."; }
  else if(pct >= 50){ verdict.innerText = "Decent foundation. Category accuracy and review recency are usually the fastest wins next."; }
  else if(pct > 0){ verdict.innerText = "Significant gaps. Start with your primary category, the single strongest ranking signal."; }
  else { verdict.innerText = "Select an option for each factor to calculate your score."; }
}
