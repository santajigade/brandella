Consent manager registration under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act is now open. In a [Press Information Bureau release dated November 14, 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2336151), the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) said the registration framework for Consent Managers came into effect on November 13, 2026, and that MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan launched an online application portal.

This matters to every Indian brand that collects leads, newsletter sign ups, app users or customer data for ads. Consent Managers are the new, registered middlemen that will let people give, manage and withdraw consent across many businesses from one place.

For marketers, it is the first practical sign that the DPDP rules are moving from paper to systems. The bigger obligations for businesses arrive in May 2027, which leaves a short window to get consent records in order.

![Consent manager registration key facts at a glance](/images/infographics/ig-consent-manager-registration-dpdp-india-1.webp)

## What a Consent Manager is under the DPDP Act

The PIB release describes a Consent Manager as an entity registered with the Data Protection Board of India that helps Data Principals (the people whose data is collected) manage their consent through an accessible, transparent and interoperable platform. The release adds that Consent Managers are accountable to Data Principals.

In plain marketing terms, imagine a customer who has opted in to emails from a bank, a retailer and an edtech app. Instead of hunting through three websites, that person could use one Consent Manager to see and change those choices. Your business (the Data Fiduciary) would then need to respect what comes through that channel.

## Who can apply for consent manager registration

The eligibility rules in the release are short and strict:

| Requirement | What the release says |
|---|---|
| Company type | Only companies incorporated in India |
| Capacity | Adequate technical, operational and financial capacity |
| Net worth | Minimum Rs 2 crore |
| Where to apply | Online portal at dpbi.gov.in |
| Who decides | The Data Protection Board of India, which may register qualifying companies |
| Public list | Registered Consent Managers will be listed on the Board's website |

[PPC Land's analysis of the announcement](https://ppc.land/india-opens-consent-manager-registration-with-a-2-crore-net-worth-floor/) points out that the conditions sit in the First Schedule of the DPDP Rules, 2025. It also reports that a government lawyer told the Kerala High Court on October 7, 2026, that the Board was constituted but not yet functional. Treat timelines for actual approvals as unconfirmed until the Board publishes its first list.

## Key DPDP dates marketers should note

The release confirms two dates that matter for planning:

- **November 13, 2026:** the Consent Manager registration framework took effect.
- **May 13, 2027:** provisions on Data Principal rights, Data Fiduciary obligations, and the Board's inquiry and penalty processes take effect.

That second date is the one to circle. It is when duties such as honouring rights requests become enforceable for businesses, according to the release. The release points readers to [meity.gov.in](https://www.meity.gov.in/) for the full DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.

## What marketers should do now

![Checklist for DPDP consent manager readiness](/images/infographics/ig-consent-manager-registration-dpdp-india-2.webp)

Most marketing teams will not become Consent Managers. You are far more likely to receive consent signals from one. These steps are Brandella's recommendations, not legal advice, so confirm details with your legal or privacy team.

1. **Map every consent touchpoint.** List lead forms, newsletter boxes, WhatsApp opt ins, app sign ups and checkout pages that collect personal data.
2. **Record consent properly.** For each person, keep when they consented, for what purpose and on which page. A spreadsheet column that just says "yes" will not be enough.
3. **Make withdrawal easy.** Check that unsubscribe links, preference centres and app settings work and actually stop data use downstream, including ad audiences.
4. **Audit data shared with ad platforms.** Customer lists uploaded to Google Ads, Meta or LinkedIn should only include people whose consent covers that use. Our guide to [first-party data and privacy](/data-privacy-and-first-party-data/) explains why this data is so valuable to protect.
5. **Ask your CRM and CMP vendors about Consent Manager support.** If customers start using registered Consent Managers, your tools will need a way to receive and apply those signals.
6. **Watch the Board's public list.** Once registered Consent Managers appear on the Board's website, check whether your vendors or partners are among them.
7. **Plan a budget line for 2027.** Consent and preference management work often needs developer time, so raise it in this quarter's planning.

## How this connects to consent tools you already use

Many Indian sites already run cookie banners for Google's Consent Mode. That is a good base, but it is not the same thing. Consent Mode passes cookie consent to Google tags, while the DPDP framework covers consent for personal data in general, across channels. ## FAQ about consent manager registration

### When did consent manager registration start in India?

The framework came into effect on November 13, 2026, and MeitY announced the online portal at dpbi.gov.in on November 14, 2026.

### What net worth does a Consent Manager need?

The PIB release says applicants need a minimum net worth of Rs 2 crore, plus adequate technical, operational and financial capacity.

### Can a foreign company become a Consent Manager?

According to the release, only companies incorporated in India can apply.

### Do marketers need to register as Consent Managers?

No. Registration is for companies that want to run consent platforms. Most brands are Data Fiduciaries and need to respect consent, not register.

### When do DPDP obligations for businesses apply?

The release says provisions on Data Principal rights, Data Fiduciary obligations and the Board's penalty processes take effect on May 13, 2027.
