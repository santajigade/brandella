OpenAI launched Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on November 10, 2026. It gives companies two new tools: a Business Hub, where they can claim and manage how their brand appears in ChatGPT, and Business Agents, chat assistants that answer shopper questions on the brand's behalf.

The official announcement is on [OpenAI's site](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/). [Reuters reported](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/openai-lets-businesses-claim-chatgpt-profiles-adds-ai-agents-for-us-e-commerce-firms-ce785edad188f725) that businesses can claim their profile, update their information and track how users engage with them, while Business Agents start with eligible ecommerce and online service businesses in the United States.

For marketers, this is the first time ChatGPT offers something close to a Google Business Profile: a place to correct your details and see how often the assistant mentions you. Here is what is in the beta, what is still limited, and what to do this week.

![Businesses in ChatGPT: Business Hub vs Business Agents at a glance](/images/infographics/ig-businesses-in-chatgpt-business-hub-agents-1.webp)

## What Business Hub does

Business Hub is the management side. According to Reuters, companies can claim their business in ChatGPT and, after verification, update key details and add product feeds with pricing and availability.

[The Next Web reported](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-businesses-in-chatgpt-business-agents) that verified companies get a blue check, and that the hub shows how often ChatGPT mentions a business, how many users click through to its website, and common conversation topics. [Unite.AI noted](https://www.unite.ai/openai-launches-business-hub-and-business-agents-in-chatgpt/) that claiming a profile does not guarantee higher placement or more sales, and that claiming is limited to eligible businesses with a website.

The data is aggregated. Reuters says insights include mentions, views and website clicks, and only appear once interactions pass certain thresholds. OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses.

## How Business Agents work

When a business shows up in a ChatGPT answer, a user can open its profile and start a chat with that business's agent. The Next Web says the agent answers using the company's own product details, and that these chats are labeled and kept separate from ChatGPT's own answers.

The same report lists the rules agents must follow: they must disclose that they are AI, keep prices accurate and avoid pressure tactics. Purchases and sharing personal data need the user's explicit approval. For now, buyers are sent to the brand's own website to complete a purchase.

| Feature | Business Hub | Business Agents |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Claimed, verified brand profile | Branded chat assistant inside ChatGPT |
| Who can use it now | Eligible businesses with a website | Eligible US ecommerce and online service businesses |
| What you control | Key details, product feeds, prices, availability | Answers based on your product details |
| What you get back | Aggregated mentions, views, clicks, topics | Labeled chats, users sent to your site |

## How Businesses in ChatGPT connects to ChatGPT Ads

There is a paid layer too. Reuters says businesses can sponsor their Business Agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT Ads, and sponsored placements are labeled. The Next Web says this builds on Sponsored Agents, which OpenAI began testing in September, and cites Digiday reporting that paying for this placement does not change how often ChatGPT recommends a business.

If you already run ChatGPT Ads, this gives you a new landing option besides your website. Our recent post on [ChatGPT Ads target CPA and target CPC](/chatgpt-ads-target-cpa-target-cpc/) covers the bidding side.

## Who can get access first

Reuters lists two faster paths. Existing OpenAI Enterprise customers and advertisers can claim their business through their OpenAI representative. Shopify merchants can connect their stores through Business Hub or the Shopify admin.

Everyone else should expect a gradual rollout. Unite.AI reports that OpenAI plans to expand to more markets and business categories, but no dates or country list have been published. Brands in India and other markets outside the US cannot launch a Business Agent yet.

## What marketers should do now

These steps are Brandella's recommendations, not official OpenAI instructions.

![Six steps to prepare your brand for Businesses in ChatGPT](/images/infographics/ig-businesses-in-chatgpt-business-hub-agents-2.webp)

1. **Check how ChatGPT describes you today.** Ask ChatGPT about your brand, your top products and your category. Note wrong prices, old addresses or missing products. Our guide on [how ChatGPT chooses websites for answers](/how-chatgpt-chooses-websites-for-answers/) explains where that information comes from.
2. **Claim your profile when it is offered.** If you are eligible, claim and verify early so you control your key details. If you work with an OpenAI representative or sell on Shopify, ask about access now.
3. **Clean your product feed.** Business Hub takes pricing and availability through product feeds, so fix errors and out of stock items first. The same discipline helps on every shopping channel.
4. **Keep your facts consistent everywhere.** Use the same name, address, phone and product details on your site, your Google profile and directories. Our post on [NAP consistency](/nap-consistency-hidden-local-seo-ranking-factor/) shows how.
5. **Plan your agent's answers.** Write clear, accurate answers to the top 20 questions shoppers ask before buying. These also make strong FAQ content for your website.
6. **Track the new numbers.** Once insights appear, record ChatGPT mentions and website clicks each month next to your Google data, so you can see if AI discovery is growing for your brand.

## Why this matters beyond ChatGPT

This launch shows AI assistants moving from reading the open web to taking structured data straight from brands. That makes accurate, verified business information a marketing asset in its own right, not only an SEO task.

Our view: treat ChatGPT like another discovery channel with its own listing. The brands that keep their data accurate and their answers helpful will be in the best position as OpenAI widens access.
