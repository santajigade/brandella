Sponsored Products ads on Audible are now live in the US. In a [What's New announcement dated November 9, 2026](https://advertising.amazon.com/resources/whats-new/sponsored-product-ads-on-audible), Amazon Ads says Sponsored Products ads for audiobooks are in open beta on Audible, and that advertisers are not charged for ads that serve there during the beta.

The change affects publishers, vendors and authors who already promote audiobooks with Sponsored Products. Amazon says active audiobook ads can serve on Audible under existing bids and targeting, and "No additional action is needed" for them to appear.

That sounds like free reach, and in a way it is. But it also changes how your reports read, which matters if anyone judges your campaigns on ROAS or cost of sales.

![Sponsored Products ads on Audible key facts](/images/infographics/ig-sponsored-products-ads-on-audible-beta-1.webp)

## How Sponsored Products ads on Audible work

Here is what Amazon's announcement confirms:

| Detail | What Amazon says |
|---|---|
| Market | US, open beta |
| Ad type | Sponsored Products ads for audiobooks |
| Setup | No opt in; active audiobook ads can serve with existing bids and targeting |
| Cost on Audible | Not charged during the open beta |
| Cost elsewhere | Audiobook ads on Amazon Store or elsewhere are charged as usual |
| Reporting | Grouped under the "Other on Amazon" placement |
| Ad label and click | "Sponsored" label, links to the book's Audible detail page |
| Who can use it | Vendors (managed service and self service) and authors |
| Access | Amazon Ads Agent and the Amazon Ads API |

Amazon frames the Audible detail page link as a way to keep customers in the familiar Audible purchase flow. For listeners, that means fewer steps between seeing an ad and buying the audiobook.

## Reporting changes to watch

Two reporting details deserve attention. First, Audible traffic does not get its own placement line. It is folded into "Other on Amazon", so you cannot easily split Audible clicks from other placements in that bucket.

Second, Amazon says attributed sales for audiobook ads that delivered impressions before November 9, 2026, may change, because they now include conversions from Audible purchases. In other words, past numbers can be restated.

[PPC Land's write up of the launch](https://ppc.land/amazon-puts-sponsored-products-ads-inside-audible-free-during-beta/) also notes that the [UK English version of Amazon's page](https://advertising.amazon.com/en-gb/resources/whats-new/sponsored-product-ads-on-audible) gives a different restatement date, November 2, 2026. If you manage both markets' reporting, check the version you rely on. PPC Land's view is that free Audible clicks plus newly counted Audible sales could make ROAS look stronger than it will once Audible clicks are billed. That is a reasonable caution, and we agree it is worth planning for.

## What audiobook advertisers should do now

![Checklist for Sponsored Products ads on Audible](/images/infographics/ig-sponsored-products-ads-on-audible-beta-2.webp)

These steps are Brandella's recommendations based on the announcement.

1. **Annotate November 9, 2026, in every report.** Anyone comparing weeks before and after should know that the inventory and attribution rules changed.
2. **Export a baseline now.** Save your audiobook campaign metrics for the last 30 days, so you have a clean comparison point if numbers are restated.
3. **Watch the "Other on Amazon" line.** A sudden rise in impressions or sales there is the clearest sign your ads are serving on Audible.
4. **Do not raise bids just because ROAS jumps.** Some of the lift may come from free Audible clicks. Hold bids steady for a couple of weeks, then decide.
5. **Check your audiobook detail pages on Audible.** The ad sends listeners straight there, so cover art, sample, description and reviews need to be strong.
6. **Tell authors and clients what changed.** If you manage campaigns for authors, explain that better numbers during the beta may not last when billing starts.
7. **Plan for the end of the beta.** Amazon has not given an end date. Set a reminder to review budgets when Audible billing begins.

Our guide to [attribution models](/attribution-models-explained-digital-marketers/) is a useful refresher if your team needs to explain why restated numbers are not the same as better performance.

## FAQ about Sponsored Products ads on Audible

### Do I need to opt in to show ads on Audible?

No. Amazon says active audiobook ads can serve on Audible under existing bidding and targeting, with no additional action needed.

### Are Audible ad clicks charged during the beta?

Amazon says advertisers are not charged for ads that serve on Audible during the open beta. Ads on Amazon Store or elsewhere are charged as usual.

### Where do Audible results show in reports?

They are aggregated under the "Other on Amazon" placement in Sponsored Products reporting.

### Who can run Sponsored Products ads on Audible?

Amazon lists vendors, including managed service and self service advertisers, and authors.

### Is this available outside the US?

The announcement covers the US open beta only.
