Meta Ads Manager now offers a 6-second ThruPlay option for video campaigns. Advertisers who choose the "Maximize ThruPlay views" goal can set the minimum watch time that counts as a ThruPlay to 6 seconds, alongside the familiar 15-second default.

The change was first shared on November 16, 2026 by Bram Van der Hallen of Edge.be in a [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bramvanderhallen_new-you-can-now-optimize-thruplays-for-share-7528016836924194817-4tha/), and reported the same day by [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/six-seconds-can-now-count-as-a-watched-video-ad-on-meta/). PPC Land notes that Meta has not published a formal announcement, though the Ads Manager notice links to a help page titled "About 6-second ThruPlays."

For anyone buying video on Facebook and Instagram, this changes what a "view" means in your reports. It can lower cost per ThruPlay on short videos, but it also makes old and new numbers hard to compare.

![Infographic comparing the 15-second and 6-second ThruPlay options in Meta Ads Manager](/images/infographics/ig-meta-6-second-thruplay-option-1.webp)

## What the 6-second ThruPlay setting does

ThruPlay has long been Meta's video view goal. A ThruPlay counted when someone watched 15 seconds of a video, or watched it to the end if it was shorter. The new setting lets you lower that bar.

Here is what has been reported about the new field, based on the PPC Land coverage and the [ads.expert ThruPlay glossary](https://ads.expert/glossary/thruplay), which quotes Meta's help text.

| Detail | 15-second ThruPlay | 6-second ThruPlay |
|---|---|---|
| When a view counts | 15 seconds watched, or full play for shorter videos | 6 seconds watched |
| Very short videos | Full play | Up to 97% completion for clips under 6 seconds |
| Objectives | Awareness and Engagement | Awareness and Engagement |
| Buying types | Auction and reservation | Auction and reservation |
| Suggested formats | Longer in feed video | Short form such as Reels and Stories |

The field in Ads Manager is called "ThruPlay duration" and carries the instruction "Choose the minimum watch duration that counts as a ThruPlay." The dropdown offers 6 and 15 seconds.

## What Meta has not said yet

There is no official rollout date, market list or eligibility rule in the public coverage. PPC Land also says it is unclear how reporting columns will treat the shorter view, for example whether ThruPlay results from 6 and 15-second ad sets will sit in the same column.

Until Meta publishes more, treat the feature as rolling out gradually. If you do not see the field yet, check again over the next few weeks rather than assuming it is not available in your country.

## Why the 6-second ThruPlay matters for short video

Most short video is built to land its message fast. A 15-second goal on a 9-second Reel only counted full plays, so the optimisation target was strict for creative that never needed 15 seconds.

A 6-second goal gives Meta's delivery system a bar that fits short creative. That is likely why Meta's interface points the shorter duration at Reels and Stories. Our guide to [creative video marketing ideas](/7-creative-video-marketing-ideas/) covers how to front load the message for formats like these.

The trade off is depth of attention. Six seconds is enough for a hook and a brand moment, but not for a product demo. If your video needs time to explain, a 15-second goal is still the safer choice.

## What advertisers should do now

These are Brandella's recommendations, not official Meta guidance.

![Checklist of five steps to test the 6-second ThruPlay option](/images/infographics/ig-meta-6-second-thruplay-option-2.webp)

1. **Check your Awareness and Engagement ad sets.** Open the performance goal and look for the ThruPlay duration field.
2. **Run a clean split.** Duplicate one ad set, set one copy to 6 seconds and keep the other at 15, with the same creative, audience and budget.
3. **Judge on outcomes, not cost per ThruPlay.** A 6-second view is cheaper by definition. Compare reach, brand lift, follow on engagement and site visits instead. Our [paid media reporting dashboard](/paid-media-reporting-dashboard/) guide shows how to keep these side by side.
4. **Label your reports.** Add the ThruPlay duration to campaign names, such as "TP6" or "TP15", so nobody mixes the two in a monthly report.
5. **Match creative to the goal.** Use 6 seconds for short Reels and Stories cut downs, and keep longer explainers on 15 seconds.

## FAQ about the 6-second ThruPlay

### Is the 15-second ThruPlay going away?

No. Based on the reported dropdown, 15 seconds remains available and 6 seconds is an added option.

### Which campaigns can use a 6-second ThruPlay?

Reports say Awareness and Engagement objectives, for both auction and reservation buying.

### Has Meta officially announced the change?

Not with a public announcement so far. The option appears in Ads Manager with a link to a help page.

### Will a 6-second ThruPlay lower my costs?

It may lower cost per ThruPlay because the bar is lower, but that does not mean the media is more effective. Test it against your real goals.
